ORDER
The Court, after considering the complaint, the record, the applicable law, the Magistrate
Judge’s Report and Recommendation, and finding that as of this date the parties have filed no
objections to the Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation, hereby approves the Magistrate
Judge’s Report and Recommendation and adopts it as its opinion.  Accordingly,
 IT IS ORDERED  that Plaintiff’s claims are  DISMISSED under 28 U.S.C.
§ 1915(e)(2)(B) for failure to state a claim as to the named defendants and for lack of subject -
matter jurisdiction as to any claims against Bill Hood Auto Group and Ford.1
