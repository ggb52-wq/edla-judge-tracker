ORDER
 Plaintiff Calvin Brown, proceeding pro se, brings this action under 42
U.S.C. § 1983, alleging that he has been denied adequate medical care
because prison officials at the Tangipahoa Parish Jail have not timely
presented him for dialysis for his chronic kidney and heart conditions .1  On
December 21, 2023, Magistrate Judge Karen Wells Roby  issued a Report &
Recommendation (“R&R”), recommending that the Court dismiss plaintiff’s
complaint pursuant to Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 41(b), for failure to
prosecute or comply with a court order.2
 Plaintiff did not object to the R&R.  Therefore, th e Court reviews the
R&R for clear error.  Douglass v. United Servs. Auto. Ass’n, 79 F.3d 1415,
1430 (5th Cir. 1996); see also Fed. R. Civ. P. 72(b) advisory committee’s note
(1983) (“When no timely objecti on is filed, the court need only satisfy itself
