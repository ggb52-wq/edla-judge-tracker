 This case is an appeal of the  C ommissioner of the Social Security
Administration’s (the “Commissioner”) denial of plaintiff Shena Carpenter’s
claim for supplemental security income (“SSI”).  On December 31, 2021 ,
plaintiff filed a motion for summary judgment. 1  On February 2, 2022, the
Commissioner filed a cross -motion for summary judgment. 2  On June 17,
2022, Magistrate Judge Janis van Meerveld issued a Report and
Recommendation (“R&R”), recommending that the Court grant plaintiff’s
motion, deny defendant’s cross-motion, and remand plaintiff’s case to the
Commissioner for further proceedings in accordance with the R&R.3
 No party objected to the R&R.  Therefore, the Court reviews the R&R
for clear error.  Douglass v. United Servs. Auto. Ass’n , 79 F.3d 1415, 1430
(5th Cir. 1996); se e also  Fed. R. Civ. P. 72(b) advisory committee’s note

 Accordingly, plaintiff’s motion for summary judgment is GRANTED,

Court remands this case to the Commissioner for further proceedings in
