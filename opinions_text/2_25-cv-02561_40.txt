ORDER
 The Court, having considered the complaint, the record, the applicable law, the Report and
Recommendation of the United States Magistrate Judge, and the Plaintiff’s timely filed objection
(R. Doc. 37), hereby approves the Report and Recommendation of the United States Magistrate
Judge and adopts it as its opinion in this matter, overruling Plaintiff's objections.
Therefore,
IT IS ORDERED  that Plaintiff Deninah Goodwin ’s claims against Defendan ts Luciola
Diaz, Joni Filfault, and Dennisse Calcano are DISMISSED WITH PREJUDICE  for failure to
prosecute.
IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that Plaintiff’s claims against Defendants Angele Rogers,
Roxanne Hernandez, and Isabel Hernandez are DISMISSED WITHOUT PREJUDICE  for
failure to serve.
IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that Plaintiff’s request to amend the complaint is DENIED
for failure to state a claim for which relief may be granted.
