ORDER
The Court, having considered the record, the applicable law, the Report and
Recommendation1 of the United States Magistrate Judge, and the failure 2 of the
Plaintiff Norris Joseph Foret to file an objection to the Magistrate Judge’s Report and
Recommendation, hereby approves the Report and Recommendation of the
Magistrate Judge and adopts it as its opinion in this matter.
Accordingly,
IT IS ORDERED  that Plaintiff’s complaint be DISMISSED WITHOUT
PREJUDICE for failure to prosecute or comply with this Court’s order.
New Orleans, Louisiana, this 24th day of January, 2025.
BRANDON S. LONG
UNITED STATES DISTRICT JUDGE
