ORDER

 Plaintiff Madeleine Dixon filed an unopposed motion for
reimbursement of service expenses. 1  On November 27, 2024, Magistrate
Judge Eva J. Dossier issued a Re port and Recommendation (“R&R”),
recommending that the Court grant plaintiff’s motion and order Defendants
Taqueria LA 20, LLC and Sean Toups to jointly pay plaintiff the total sum of
$1,025.00.2  Defendants did not object to the R&R.  Therefore, the Court
reviews the R&R for clear error.  See Douglass v. United Servs. Auto. Ass’n,
79 F.3d 1415, 1430 (5th Cir. 1996) (en banc), superseded by statute on other
grounds, 28 U.S.C. § 636(b)(1); see also  Fed. R. Civ. P. 72(b) advisory
committee’s note (1983) (“When no timely objection is filed, the court need
only satisfy itself that there is no clear error on the face of the record in order
to accept the recommendation.”).  The Court finds no clear error.
