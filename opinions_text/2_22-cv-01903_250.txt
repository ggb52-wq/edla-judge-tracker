Defendants filed a “Response to Plaintiff’s Improper Submission” docketed as a Motion to
Strike.  ECF No. 245.
A motion to strike is governed by Rule 12(f) of the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure, which
provides that “[t]he court may strike from a pleading an insufficient defense or any redundant,
immaterial, impertinent, or scandalous matter.”  By its own terms, a motion to strike is directed to
pleadings, which Rule 7 defines as the complaint, answer, answer to a counterclaim, answer to a
crossclaim, third-party complaint, and reply to an answer.  FED. R. CIV. P. 7(a).  Although the Fifth
Circuit has not definitively addressed whether a Rule 12(f) motion targeting a brief is appropriate
in light of Rule 7(a) ,1 other circuits have limited Rule 12(f) motions to pleadings to deny Rule
12(f) motions directed to motions, briefs or memoranda.2  Commentators and numerous decisions
from this district and other districts are in accord. 3  As Plaintiff’s filing is not a “pleading” under
