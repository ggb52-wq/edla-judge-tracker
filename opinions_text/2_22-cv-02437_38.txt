ORDER
 On March 2, 202 3, plaintiff Oil States International, Inc.  filed a n
unopposed motion for attorney’s fees, seeking a total of $17,107.00 in fees
and costs.1  On July 6, 2023, Magistrate Judge Janis van Meerveld  issued a
Report and Recommendation (“R&R”), recommending that the Court grant
plaintiff’s motion and award attorney’s fees and costs in the amount of
$17,107.00.2
 No party objected to the R&R.  Therefore, the Court reviews the R&R
for clear error.  See Douglass v. United Servs. Auto. Ass’n, 79 F.3d 1415, 1430
(5th Cir. 1996) (en banc), superseded by statute on other grounds, 28 U.S.C.
§ 636(b)(1); see also Fed. R. Civ. P. 72(b) advisory committee’s note (1983)
(“When no timely objection is filed, the court need only satisfy itself that
there is no clear error on the face of the record in order to accept the
recommendation.”).  The Court finds no clear error.
