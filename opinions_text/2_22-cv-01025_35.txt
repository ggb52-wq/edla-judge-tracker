ORDER1
Before the Court is the parties’ Joint Stipulation Regarding Transfer
(Doc. 34).  All parties agree the Eastern District of Louisiana  should hear this
action.  So they ask the Court to stay the pending deadlines and transfer.  The
Court agrees this is in the interest of justice and more convenient for the
parties and witnesses.  It, therefore, grants the request.  28 U.S.C. § 1404(a).
In doing so, the Court notes it s appreciation of  counsel’s ability to work
together on this matter.
