REPORT AND RECOMMENDATION

 On March 23, 2023, Defendant filed its Unopposed Motion to Compel Arbitration and
Stay Proceeding.  (Rec. doc. 7).  After confirming with counsel for Plaintiff that Plaintiff does
not oppose the motion and having reviewed the arbitration agreement (rec. doc. 7 -2)
