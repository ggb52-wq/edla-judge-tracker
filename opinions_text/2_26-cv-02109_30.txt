ORDER & REASONS
Before the Court is an emergency motion to stay the November 16, 2026 response deadline,
expedite consideration of de fendant’s motion for relief from order, reconsideration, and
evidentiary hearing, and for other appropriate relief (the “emergency motion”) filed by defendant
Mohamed Zaghia.1  Zaghia’s motion pertains to Lycée Français International de la Louisiane’s
(“Lycée Français”) motion for preliminary injunction2 filed on September 21, 2026, and his motion
for reconsideration3 filed on November 2, 2026.  The Court pr eviously granted an extension to
November 16 for Zaghia to respond to Lycée Français’s motion for preliminary injunction,4 which
is currently set for submission on November 23, 2026.  Having considered Zaghia’s memoranda,
the record, and the applicable law, the Court takes up the emergency motion on an expedited basis
but then denies the motion.
  It is well-settled that a “[ d]istrict [c]ourt has broad discretion to stay proceedings as an
incident to its power to control its own docket,” Clinton v. Jones, 520 U.S. 681, 706 (1997), “with
economy of time and effort” not only “for itself, [but also] for counsel, and for litigants.”  Landis
v. N. Am. Co., 299 U.S. 248, 254 (1936).  Furthermore, “[w] hen an act … must be done within a

injunction and has not demonstrated sufficient good cause for this Court to grant him further relief.

IT IS ORDERED that Zaghia’s emergency motion (R. Doc. 29) is DENIED.
