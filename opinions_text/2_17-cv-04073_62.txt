ORDER & REASONS
Before the Court is a Daubert motion in limine to exclude the general causation opinions
of plaintiff’s medical expert Dr. Jerald Cook filed by defendan ts BP Exploration & Production
Inc., BP America Production Company, BP p.l.c., Halliburton Energy Services, Inc., Transocean
Holdings LLC, Transocean Deepwater, Inc., an d Transocean Offshore Deepwater Drilling, Inc.
(collectively, “Defendants”).1  Plaintiff Michael Minor responds in opposition, 2 and Defendants
reply in further support of their motion.3
Also before the Court is Defendants’ motion for summary judgment in which they argue
that the case should be dismissed because Minor  cannot prove general causation without Cook’s
opinions.4  Minor responds in opposition,  5  and Defendants reply in further support of their
motion.6

 Defendants’ motions here are nearly identical  to those filed by Defendants, and granted by
this Court, in other B3 cases.  See Carpenter v. BP Expl. & Prod., Inc. , No. 17-3645, R. Doc. 64

is GRANTED, and Minor’s claims against them are DISMISSED WITH PREJUDICE.

oil-spill workers is irrelevant to the resolution of these motions.
