UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT
EASTERN DISTRICT OF LOUISIANA

ORDER
 H aving considered the complaint, the record, the applicable law, the Report and
Recommendation (“R&R”) of the United States Magistrate Judge,1 and the failure of any party to
file an objection to the R&R , the Court hereby approves the R&R and adopts it as its opinion in
this matter , except that the first sentence of the second paragraph on page 24 of the R&R is
modified to read as follows: “ For the foregoing reasons, the state courts’ decision rejecting his
claim was not contrary to , or did not involve an unreasonable application of , clearly established
federal law, as determined by the Supreme Court of the United States.”  Accordingly,
 IT IS ORDERED that the petition of James Dewitt McIntosh, II, for issuance of a writ of
habeas corpus under 28 U.S.C. § 2254 is DISMISSED WITH PREJUDICE.
New Orleans, Louisiana, this 16th day of January, 2024.
