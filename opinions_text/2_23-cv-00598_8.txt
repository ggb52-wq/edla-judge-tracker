defendants Judge Marla M. Abel, District Attorney Kristine Ru ssell, and Assistant District
Attorney Allie Fournet be DISMISSED WITH PREJUDICE  pursuant to 28 U.S.C.
§ 1915(e)(2)(B) and § 1915A(b) as frivolous, for fa ilure to state a claim for which relief can be
granted, and/or for seeking relief against immune defendants.
