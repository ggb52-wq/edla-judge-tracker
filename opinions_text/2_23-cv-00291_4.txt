ORDER AND REASONS
 This matter arises from plaintiff’s cause of action to evict defendant in
state-court proceedings. 1  Defendant removed this case on January 23,
2023.2  She then filed an ex parte motion for leave  to proceed in forma
pauperis, which Magistrate Judge Janice van Meerveld denied.3  Magistrate
Judge van Meerveld also provided a report and recommendation (“R&R”),
recommending that this matter be remanded if defendant did not pay the
filing fee within 14 days.4  Plaintiff did not object to the R&R or pay the filing
fee.  Therefore, this Court reviews the R&R for clear  error.  See Douglass v.
United Servs. Auto. Ass ’n, 79 F.3d 1415, 1430 (5th Cir. 1996) (en banc) ,
superseded by statute on other grounds, 28 U.S.C. § 636(b)(1); see also Fed.
R. Civ. P. 72(b) advisory committee’s note (1983) (“When no timely objection
is filed, the court need only satisfy itself that there is no clear error on the
