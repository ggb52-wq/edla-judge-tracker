Given the Court’s Order and Reasons (Rec. Doc. 49) dismissing the claims of
plaintiffs Devon Wells, Becky Jackson, and The Right Touch, Inc. of Hammond, and with

for Summary Judgment on Bad Faith Claims (Rec. Doc. 39), Motion to Exclude Experts

Compel and for Sanctions (Rec. Doc. 46) remains pending before Magistrate Judge Michael
North, with resolution of same directed no later than July 13, 2026.
IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that the final pretrial conference and trial dates in  the
above-captioned matter are VACATED.
