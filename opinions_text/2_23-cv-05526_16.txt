ORDER

The Court, having considered the petition, the record, the applicable law and
the Report and Recommendation of the United States Magistrate Judge, and the
failure of any party to file any objection to the Magistrate Judge’ s Report and
Recommendation, hereby approves the Report and Recommendation of the United
States Magistrate Judge and adopts it as its own opinion.  Accordingly,
  IT IS ORDERED  that the federal habeas corpus application filed by
Petitioner Skylleur Hayes is DISMISSED WITH PREJUDICE as untimely.
IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that a certificate of appealability shall not be
issued because Petitioner  has not made a substantial showing of the denial of a
constitutional right.
