ORDER AND REASONS
Before the court are the Motion to Quash Jury Demand (Rec. Doc. 11) and Rule 12(c)
Motion to Dismiss Plaintiff's Extra-Contractual Claims and for Dismissal of National
General Insurance Company (Rec. Doc. 12), filed by defendants, Imperial Fire & Casualty
Insurance Company and National General Insurance Company.
Counsel for plaintiff, Michael D. O'Brien, has informed the court that he does not oppose
the motions. Further, it appears to the court that the motions have merit. Accordingly,
IT IS HEREBY ORDERED that the motions are GRANTED;
IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that plaintiff's extra-contractual claims are DISMISSED;
IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that plaintiff's claims against National General Insurance
Company are DISMISSED.
