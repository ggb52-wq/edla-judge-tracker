ORDER
 Plaintiff’s complaint alleging claims under 42 U.S.C. § 1983 was
referred to Magistrate Michael North for a Report and Recommendation
(“R&R”).  Magistrate Judge North  subsequently recommended transfer of
this matter to the United States District Court for the Western District of
Louisiana because venue is improper as to the Eastern District of Louisiana.1
Plaintiff did not object to the R&R.  Therefore, this Court reviews the R&R
for clear error.  See Douglass v. United Servs. Auto. Ass’n, 79 F.3d 1415, 1430
(5th Cir. 1996) (en banc), superseded by statute on other grounds, 28 U.S.C.
§ 636(b)(1); see also Fed. R. Civ. P. 72(b) advisory committee’s note (1983)
(“When no timely objection is filed, the court need only satisfy itself that
there is no clear error on the face of the record in order to accept the
recommendation.”).  The Court finds no clear error.  Therefore , the Court
adopts Magistrate North’s R&R as its opinion.
