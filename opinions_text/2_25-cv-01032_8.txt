ORDER

The Court, having considered the petition, the record, the applicable law and
the Report and Recommendation of the United States Magistrate Judge (Rec. Doc. 7),
and the failure of any party to file any objection to the Magistrate Judge’s Report and
Recommendation, hereby approves the Report and Recommendation of the United
States Magistrate Judge and adopts it as its own opinion.  Accordingly,
  IT IS HEREBY ORDERED that Defendant Brandon Bell’s federal civil
rights claims be DISMISSED WITHOUT PREJUDICE  as frivolous and/or for
failure to state a claim on which relief may be granted.
