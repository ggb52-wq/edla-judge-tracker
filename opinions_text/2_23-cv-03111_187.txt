ORDER AND REASONS
 Before the Court  in this civil -rights case is the Rule 12(b)(6) motion 2 of
Defendant Orleans Parish District Attorney (“OPDA”) Jason Williams to dismiss the
official-capacity 42 U.S.C. § 1983 claims that Plaintiffs Kunta Gable and Sidney Hill
assert in their First Amended Complaint.3 Plaintiffs concede that, as pled, they have
not stated a plausible conspiracy claim against Williams. 4 However, in their
opposition to Williams’ motion, Plaintiffs maintain that they have stated a plausible
municipal-liability claim for OPDA’s deliberately-indifferent Brady policies resulting
in wrongful convictions.5 In reply, Williams counters that Plaintiffs fail to adequately
allege a pattern of constitutional violations that would have put OPDA’s policymaker
on notice of his constitutionally defective policies. 6 In support of dismissal and in
reply, Williams invokes this Court’s February 25, 2025 Order and Reasons finding
that lead plaintiff’s—Mr. Juluke’s—complaint failed to specifically plead a municipal
