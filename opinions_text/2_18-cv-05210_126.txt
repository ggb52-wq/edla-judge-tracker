The Court has before it a Motion filed by Defendants Shell Offshore, Inc. and Shell Oil
Company’s (collectively, “Shell”) to lift the stay of the above -captioned action for the limited
purpose of allowing it to file a Mot ion to Dismiss. R. Doc. 125. Shell asserts all claims raised
against it herein by Plaintiff Plaquemines Parish have been settled. Id. However, the Court ordered
this matter be stayed pending the Fifth Circuit’s ruling in Plaquemines Parish v. BP America Prod.
Co., understanding decision in that appeal to be dispositive of the issue raised by Plaintiff’s  yet-
pending Motion to Remand (R. Doc. 115). R. Doc. 124. The Fifth Circuit recently released
an opinion in Plaquemines Parish in which it rejected arguments made by Defendants in their
joint opposition to Plaintiff’s  Motion to Remand. R. Doc. 117; see Plaquemines Parish v. BP
America Prod. Co., 2024 WL 2746246, at *13–14 (5th Cir. May 29, 2024). Accordingly;
IT IS ORDERED that the Motion is GRANTED IN PART and that the stay of this matter
is hereby LIFTED.
IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that all parties to this action shall file, jointly or separately,
and no later than June 28, 2024, a memorandum of no more than seven pages explaining the impact
