ORDER AND REASONS
 This litigation involves an employment discrimination dispute. 1 Before the Court is
Defendant Pere Antoine, LLC’s (“Defendant”) Renewed Motion for Summary Judgment.2 On July
21, 2025, the Court entered an Order granting Defendant’s Motion for Summary Judgment and
dismissing Plaintiff Jacqueline Whitfield’s (“Plaintiff”) claims under Title VII, the Americans with
Disabilities Act, and the Equal Pay Act. 3 The Court granted Defendant leave of Court to file a
renewed motion addressing Plaintiff’s claim under 42 U.S.C § 1981.4 In the Renewed Motion for
Summary Judgment, Defendant argues Plaintiff’s claims under Section 1981 should be dismissed
as time-barred under the one-year statute of limitations as alleged discrete acts of discrimination.5
Alternatively, Defendant argues the Section 1981 c laim should b e dismissed on the merits. 6 In
response, Plaintiff states that she does not intend to pursue or litigate the Section 1981 claim. 7
