ORDER
 Plaintiff Ranell Joseph brings this action for a writ of habeas corpus
under 28 U.S.C. § 2254.1  On January 18, 2022, Magistrate Judge Janis van
Meerveld issued a Report & Recommendation (“R&R”), recommending that
plaintiff’s petition for habeas relief be dismissed with prejudice as untimely.2
 No party objected to the R&R.  Therefore, the  Court reviews the R&R
for clear error.  Douglass v. United Servs. Auto. Ass’n , 79 F.3d 1415, 1430
(5th Cir. 1996); see also  Fed. R. Civ. P. 72(b) a dvisory committee’s note
(1983) (“When no timely objection is filed, the court need only satisfy itself
that there is no clear error on the face of the record in order to accept the
recommendation.”).  The Court finds no clear error.
