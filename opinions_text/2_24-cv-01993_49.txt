ORDER AND REASONS
The Court, having considered Plaintiff  Girod Titling Trust’s Motion to Fix
Attorneys’ Fees,1 the record, the applicable law, the Report and Recommendation of
the United States Magistrate Judge,2 and the failure of Defendants to timely file any
objection to the Report and Recommendation, 3 hereby approves the Report and
Recommendation and adopts it as its opinion in this matter.
Accordingly,
 IT IS HEREBY ORDERED  that Plaintiff Girod Titling Trust’s Motion for
Attorneys’ Fees4 is GRANTED.
