Having considered the parties’ memoranda, the record, and the applicable law, the Co urt
finds that Walmart’s motion to dismiss should be granted for the same reasons as were the motions
of Microsoft and Alphabet – namely, the Court’s lack of subject-matter jurisdiction over Carlton’s
claims against Walmart for want of standing, and the complaint’s failure to state any viable claims
against Walmart.13
Accordingly, for the foregoing reasons,
IT I S ORDERED that Walmart’s motion to dismiss (R. Doc. 36) is GRANTED, and
Carlton’s claims against it are DISMISSED WITHOUT PREJUDICE.
