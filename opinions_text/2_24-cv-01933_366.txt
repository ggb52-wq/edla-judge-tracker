 Before the Court is  Plaintiffs’ Motion for Reconsideration of Summary
Judgment as to Trieu Law, LLC .1 Defendants oppose the Motion. 2  Plaintiffs filed a

applicable law, the Motion is DENIED.

trial court is free to reconsider and reverse its decision for any reason it deems
sufficient, even in the absence of new evidence or an intervening change in or

(b) The funds shall be property of the person or persons entitled to them

provided that it has fulfilled the four provisos, including subparts, as set forth in the
law. There is no summary judgment evidence of the satisfaction of those
requirements, or of any violation of that Act.

for reconsideration.
