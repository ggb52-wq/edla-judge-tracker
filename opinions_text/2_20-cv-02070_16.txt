 Before the Court is the parties’ joint motion to continue the trial and
other pretrial deadlines. 1  Trial in this matter is currently scheduled for

remain medical issues which need clarity ,” they will not be ready for the
current trial date. 3  Specifically, the parties note that , because they are
waiting to hear whether plaintiff’s physician will recommend a lumbar
