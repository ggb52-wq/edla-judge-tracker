ORDER

The Court has considered the complaint, the record, the applicable law, the Magistrate
Judge’s Findings and Recommendation, and P laintiff’s objection to the Magistrate Judge’s
Findings and Recommendation (ECF No. 9). The objection merely asks the Court to assist Plaintiff
in recovering the sums from the Social Security Administration he claims are owed to him; it does
not address in any manner the Magistrate Judge ’s Findings and Recommendation. The Court
hereby approves the Magistrate Judge’s Findings and Recommendation and adopts it as its
opinion.  Accordingly,
IT IS ORDERED that that Defendant Kilolo Kijakazi, Acting Commissioner of Social
Security’s Motion to Dismiss for Lack of Subject Matter Jurisdiction (ECF No. 7) is GRANTED
and that Plaintiff’s claims are DISMISSED WITHOUT PREJUDICE.
IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that the Motion f or Leave to Proceed in  Forma Pauperis
(ECF No. 10) is DISMISSED AS MOOT.
