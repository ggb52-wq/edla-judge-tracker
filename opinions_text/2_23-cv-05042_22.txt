ORDER
Before the Court is a motion to dismiss filed by plaintiff David Diamond, Jr. 1  Diamond
asks the Court to dismiss a trade-secrets counterclaim purportedly brought by defendant Shelton
Services, Inc. (“Shelton”) because Shelton has failed to adequately plead sufficient facts to warrant
such a claim.2  In opposition, Shelton denies asserting any trade-secrets claim and states that it has
only asserted a breach -of-contract claim. 3  In reply, Diamond acknowledges Shelton’s position
that no trade-secrets claim is asserted but nonetheless insists that his motion should be granted “to
make clear that no ‘trade secret’ or ‘other confidential information’ claims are being maintained.”4
In doing so, Diamond seeks to have this Court confine  Shelton’s counterclaim to “only pursuing
one claim (a breach of the Paragraph 12 of the Agreement),” 5 when Shelton has plainly asserted
its breach-of-contract claim under both s ections 11 and 12 of the employment agreement. 6
