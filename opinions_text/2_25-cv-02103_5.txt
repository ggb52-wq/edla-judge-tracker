 ORDER

The Court, having considered the complaint, the record, the applicable law, the magistrate
judge’s Report and Recommendation (“R&R”),1 and the failure of any party to file an objection
to the R&R, hereby approves the R&R and adopts it as its opinion in this matter.  The magistrate
judge properly found that the plaintiff ignored an express Court order to remedy the deficiency in
his complaint by paying the filing fee or submitting an application to proceed in forma pauperis .
Therefore,
IT IS ORDERED that plaintiff’s complaint is DISMISSED WITHOUT PREJUDICE
for failure to prosecute or comply with a court order.
New Orleans, Louisiana, this 28th day of January, 2026.
