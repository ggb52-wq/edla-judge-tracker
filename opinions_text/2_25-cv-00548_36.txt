IV. Analysis
 First, Plaintiffs are entitled to recover  an award of reasonable attorneys’ fees pursuant to
the express terms of the  parties’ contract. The General Service Agreement between the parties
stated that  “[i]n the event that legal action is brought to enforce or interpret any term of this
Agreement, the prevailing Party will be entitled to recover, in addition to any other damages or
award, all reasonable legal costs and fees associated with the action.”55 Because the Court granted
Plaintiffs’ motion for default judgment on their breach of contract claims against CBA Home
Builders, Inc.,56 Plaintiffs are the prevailing parties within the meaning of the agreement, and thus,
Plaintiffs are entitled to recover attorneys’ fees.

 Additionally, the Court must determine whether the hours expended by Plaintiffs’ counsel

records and voluntarily reduced their hours, Plaintiffs have demonstrated reasonable billing
judgment, and their hours should not be reduced further.67

D. Expenses
 In addition to attorneys’ fees, Plaintiffs request an additional $926.50 for costs associated

E. Supplementing Costs for Judgment Enforcement
Plaintiffs also request leave to supplement their attorneys’ fee award to account for
additional costs and fees anticipated in connection with enforcing the default judgment. Although
the General Service Agreement provides for the recovery of reasonable attorneys’ fees and costs
associated with an action to enforce the Agreement, Plaintiffs have not yet identified the
additional services that may be required to enforce the judgment or provided documentation
establishing the amount of any anticipated fees or  costs. As such, the Court cannot presently
determine whether such fees and costs are recoverable under the Agreement or reasonable. The
Court therefore denies Plaintiffs’ request to supplement the award at this time, without prejudice
