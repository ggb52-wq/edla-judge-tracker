The Court, having considered the complaint, the record, the applicable law, the Report
and Recommendation of the United States Magist rate Judge, and the ob jection by plaintiff,
Matthew James Greathouse, which is hereby OVERRULED, approves the Magistrate Judge’s

Second Judicial District Co urt of Terrebonne Parish are DISMISSED	WITH	PREJUDICE for
lack of subject-matter jurisdiction and be cause the Thirty- Second Judicial Court of
Terrebonne Parish is not a juridical person capable of being sued.
IT	IS	FURTHER	ORDERED that the plaintiff’s request to file an amended
pleading pursuant to Rule 15 of the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure is GRANTED.
