UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT
EASTERN DISTRICT OF LOUISIANA
BERT PATRICK JENNINGS CIVIL ACTION
VERSUS NO. 25-1829
TEXAS STATE POWER AUTHORITY SECTION “G” (4)
ORDER
The Court, having considered the complaint, the record, the applicable law, the Report and
Recommendation of the United States Magistrate Judge, and the failure of any party to file an
objection to the Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation, hereby approves the Magistrate
Judge’s Report and Recommendation and adopts it as its opinion in this matter.  Therefore,
IT IS ORDERED  that plaintiff Bert Jennings ’s 42 U.S.C. §  1983 complaint against
defendant Texas State Power Authority be DISMISSED WITH PREJUDICE  pursuant to 28
U.S.C. § 1915, § 1915A and 42 U.S.C. § 1997e  as frivolous and for failure to state a claim for
which relief can be granted.
