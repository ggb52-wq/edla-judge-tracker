ORDER
Before the Court is the Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation
relating to Plaintiff Lanie McCann’s Motion for Attorneys’ fees (Doc. 3 6).
Plaintiff filed  objections to the Report and Recommendation on August 10,
2026. Accordingly, the Court reviews the Report and Recommendation de novo.
The Court, after considering the memoranda, the exhibits, the applicable law,
the Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation, hereby approves the
Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation and adopts it as its opinion .
The Court only wishes to emphasize the following reasoning.
In Plaintiff’s objections, she argues that the Magistrate was “undeniably
wrong” in finding that the Plaintiff’s Motion was untimely under Federal Rule
of Civil Procedure 52(d)(2)(B)(i), characterizing the Magistrate’s reasoning as

“troubling.”1 Plaintiff contends that Local Rule 54.3 supersedes Rule 52 and
allows a motion for attorney fees to be filed within thirty-five days of receiving
notice of the entry of judgment. Plaintiff’s troubles are misplaced. Local Rule
54.3 is titled “Certification of Costs” and allows a prevailing party to file a
“motion to tax costs ” within thirty-five days of receiving the notice of entry of
judgment. Attorney fees are not costs, nor are they mentioned once in Local
Rule 54.3. Plaintiff’s Motion is untimely.
Accordingly,
IT IS ORDERED that Plaintiff Lanie McCann’s Motion for Attorneys’
Fees is DENIED.
