O R D E R
The Court, having considered the complaint, the record, the applicable law, the
Report and Recommendation of the United States Magistrate Judge, and the failure
of any party to file an objection to the Magistrate Judge’ s Report and
Recommendation, hereby approves the Magistrate Judge’s Report and
Recommendation and adopts it as its opinion in this matter. Therefore,
IT IS ORDERED  that Plaintiff Robert Earl King III’s 42 U.S.C. §  1983
complaint against Defendant Redivac Emergency Transportation Service be
DISMISSED WITH PREJUDICE  pursuant to 28 U.S.C. § 1915, § 1915A , and 42
U.S.C. § 1997e  as frivolous and for failure to state a claim for which relief can be
granted.
