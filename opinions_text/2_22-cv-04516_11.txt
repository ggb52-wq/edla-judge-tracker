ORDER
The Court, after considering the Complain t, the record, the applicable law, the
Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendatio n, and finding that as of this date
Columbus Chris Williams (“Plaint iff”) has filed no objections to the Magistrate Judge’s
Report and Recommendation, 1 hereby approves the Report and Recommendation and
adopts it as its opinion.
Accordingly;
IT IS ORDERED that the petition of Columbus Chris Williams for issuance of a
writ of habeas corpus under 28 U.S.C. § 2241,  properly construed as requesting relief
under § 2254, is hereby DISMISSED WITHOUT PREJUDICE for lack of jurisdiction
and failure to establish authorization to proceed with a second or successive petition.
