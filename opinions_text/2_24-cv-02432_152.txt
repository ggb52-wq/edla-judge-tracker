other bases, her employment with and separation from Waste Management,

Adams would pay attorney’s fees if she breached the agreement. 9

II. LEGAL STANDARD
The Court applies de novo review to the parts of the R&R to which the
parties objected. Fed. R. Civ. P. 72(b)(3).  The Court is limited to plain-error
review of any part of the R&R no t subject to a proper objection .  Starns v.
Andrews, 524 F.3d 612, 617 (5th Cir. 2008).
III. DISCUSSION
