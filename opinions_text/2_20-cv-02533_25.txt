O R D E R
Having considered the complaint, the record, the applicable law, United States Magistrate
Judge’s Report and Recommendation (“R&R”), and the failure of any party to file any objections
to the R&R, the Court hereby approves the R&R and adopts it as its opinion in this matter.
Therefore,
IT IS ORDERED  that the petition of Kenneth Halley for issuance of a writ of habeas
corpus under 28 U.S.C. § 2254 be DENIED and DISMISSED WITH PREJUDICE.
New Orleans, Louisiana, this 7th day of January 2022.
