ORDER

The Court, having considered the complaint, the record, the applicable law, the Report and
Recommendation of the United States Magistrate Judge, and the failure of Plaintiff to file an
objection to the Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation, hereby approves the Magistrate
Judge’s Report and Recommendation and adopts it as its opinion herein.
On September 25, 2025, i n the Noti ce of Deficiency, R. Doc. 2, P laintiff was instructed
within 21 days either to submit the filing fee or to return the completed A pplication to Proceed
Without Paying Fees and Affidavit, which was provided to him. R. Doc. 2. Plaintiff did not do so.
On November 7, 2025, the Court issued an Order instructing Plaintiff to show cause in writing by
November 28, 2025, why his case should not be dismissed for his failure to either pay the filing
fee or submit a certified pauper application. R. Doc. 3. Plaintiff  again failed to do so. The
Magistrate Judge then issued her Report and Recommendation on December 23, 2025,
recommending that Plaintiff’s complaint be dismissed with out prejudice for failure to prosecute
his case pursuant to Fed. R. Civ. P. 41(b), namely that he had failed to pay the filing fee or submit
an application to proceed as pauper. R. Doc. 4. Plaintiff was given 14 days from the date of the
Report and Recommendation to file an objection thereto. Instead of filing an objection, paying the
filing fee, or submitting a pauper application, Plaintiff filed a “Response to the Notice of
