REPORT & RECOMMENDATION
 The above -captioned matter is an  employment discrimination action brought under 42
U.S.C. 2000e by pro se Plaintiff, Jessica L. Seamon, against Defendant Nicholls State University.
See R. Doc. 1. The Complaint was filed on November 27, 2023, and s ummons were issued and
forwarded to Ms. Seamon on November 29, 2023. Id.  See also R. Doc. 2. Ms. Seamon has not
filed any applications for leave to file this matter in forma pauperis , proof of service, or waivers
of service to date.
 On April 7, 2025, the Court ordered Ms. Seamon to show cause, in writing, by no later than
April 16, 2025, why Defendant Nicholls State University should not be dismissed for failure to
provide proof of service. R. Doc. 4. Ms. Seamon did not file any response to the Court’s Order,
nor any proof of service or waivers of service.
 Under the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure, “[i]f a defendant is not served within 90 days
after the complaint is filed, the court – on motion or on its owner after notice to the plaintiff – must
dismiss the action without prejudice against that defendant or order that service be made within a
specified time. But if plaintiff shows good cause for the failure, the court must extend the time for
service for an appropriate period.” FED. R. CIV. P. 4(m).
 It has been over a year since this lawsuit was filed, and proof of service is still lacking. The
Court hoped to bring this deficiency to Ms. Seamon’s attention by issuing a show cause Order but
there has been no response, nor any attempts of service filed into the record. Since Ms. Seamon is

proceeding in this matter pro se , these failures are attributable to her alone. See e.g. Harris v.

 Accordingly,
IT IS RECOMMENDED that Plaintiff Jessica L. Seamon’s suit be dismissed without
prejudice pursuant to Rule 4(m) of the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure.
