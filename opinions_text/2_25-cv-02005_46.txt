LAW AND ANALYSIS
Defendant Quality Process Services, LLC filed this motion  seeking leave to file a cross -
claim against co-defendants W & T Offshore, Inc. and Danos, L.L.C . on August 10, 2026.  ECF
No. 41.  The governing Scheduling Order required all amendments to pleadings be filed on or
before August 10, 2026.  ECF No. 39 at 1.  Thus, Defendant timely sought leave before expiration
of the Scheduling Order’s deadline, and the request for leave is governed by Rule 15(a) of the
Federal Rules of Civil Procedure rather than the more stringent good cause requirements of Rule
16(b).  See S & W Enters., L.L.C. v. SouthTrust Bank of Ala., NA , 315 F.3d 533, 535-36 (5th Cir.
2003) (holding Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 16(b) governs the amendment of pleadings after a

scheduling order deadline has expired and allows modification “only . . . for good cause”; the more
liberal standard of Rule 15(a) applies to the court’s decision to grant or deny leave only after the
movant demonstrates good cause to modify the scheduling order).
Under Rule 15(a)(2), the “court should freely give leave [to amend] when justice so
requires.”1  The five relevant considerations for examination by the court in determining whether
to grant leave to amend a complaint are: (1) undue delay; (2) bad faith or dilatory motive;
(3)repeated failure to cure deficiencies by previous amendments; (4) undue prejudice to the
opposing party; and (5) futility of the amendment.2  Given Rule 15(a)’s “bias in favor of granting
leave’” absent a “substantial reason,” the court’s discretion “is not broad enough to permit denial.”3
No party has filed an Opposition to Defendant’s motion.  Absent identification of any
reason for opposition to the motion, there is no “substantial reason” to deny the request for leave
under Rule 15(a).
Accordingly, for the foregoing reasons and considering the record, the submission and
argument of counsel, the lack of opposition filed by any party, the failure to demonstrate of
prejudice from granting leave to amend, and the applicable law,
IT IS ORDERED that Defendant Quality Process Services, LLC’s Motion for Leave to
File Cross Claim against Danos, LLC and W & T Offshore, Inc. is GRANTED;
