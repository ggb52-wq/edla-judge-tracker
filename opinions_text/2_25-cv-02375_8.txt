ORDER
 The Court, after considering the complaint, the record, the applicable law, the Magistrate
Judge’s Report and Recommendation, and finding that as of this date plaintiff has filed no
objections to the Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation, hereby approves the Magistrate
Judge’s Report and Recommendation and adopts it as its opinion.1 Accordingly,
IT IS ORDERED that Plaintiff Vance Scott Senior’s official capacity claims against St.
Charles Parish employees Woodruff Camus, Ken (also known as Eric)  Lorio, Michael Albert,
Marilyn Ross, Robert Raymond, Corey Oubre, and Orrin Marino and St. Charles Parish Sheriff’s
Office Deputies Jack Gilboy, Stephen Cantelli, Jason Guidry, Lane Haun, Huey Marcel, Ruche
Marino, Don Murray, Brandon Oubre are DISMISSED WITH PREJUDICE for failure to state
claims upon which relief can be granted.2
IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that Plaintiff’s purported claims based on 18 U.S.C. §§ 241
and 242 and LA. REV. STAT. §§ 14:67, 14:134, and 14:134.4,3 his Fifth Amendment due process
