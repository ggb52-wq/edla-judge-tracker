ORDER AND REASONS

Before the Court is the Motion to Dismiss Purs uant to Fed. R. Civ. P. 12(b)(5) (R. Doc.
23) filed by Defendant Chex Systems, Inc. (“Chex”). Despite being t itled “motion to dismiss ,”
Chex moves for dismissal only in the alter native. Chex first moves for an order  quashing the
purported service of process made upon Chex on September 22, 2026, and  declaring that Chex is
not required to answer or otherwise respond to the Complaint unless and until it is properly served
in accordance with Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 4, and that Chex’s time to respond shall run
from the date of valid service.1 Chex contends that on September 22, 2026, its registered agent for
service of process was served with a summons directing Chex to serve an answer to the attached
complaint within 21 days after service of the summons, but the Complaint was not attached.2 Chex
thus avers service of pr ocess was ins ufficient because Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 4(c)(1)
requires that “[a] summons must be served with a copy of the complaint.”3

pauperis in this action,  and on whose behal f service was made by the United States Mar shals
