The Court, having considered the Complaint (R. Doc. 1), the Motion to Dismiss
Pursuant to 12(B)(6) (R. Doc. 19), the record, the applicable law, the Magistrate
Judge’s Report and Recommendation (R. Doc. 23), and the lack of objections thereto,1
finds that the Report and Recommendation is neither clearly erroneous nor contrary
to law.  As such, the Court a pproves the Magistrate Judge’s Findings and
Recommendation and adopts it as its opinion.
Accordingly,
IT IS HEREBY ORDERED that the defendants’ Motion to Dismiss Pursuant
to 12(B)(6) (R. Doc. 19) is DENIED.
