ORDER
The Court, having considered the Motion for Summary judgment filed by
plaintiff, Tomarcus Porter (R. Doc. 34), the record, the applicable law, the Magistrate
Judge’s Report and Recommendation (R. Doc. 43), and the lack of objections thereto,
finds that the Report and Recommendation is neither clearly erroneous nor contrary
to law.  As such, the Court a pproves the Magistrate Judge’s Findings and
Recommendation and adopts it as its opinion.
Accordingly,
IT IS HEREBY ORDERED that Plaintiffs’ Motion for Summary Judgment
(R. Doc. 34) is DISMISSED WITHOUT PREJUDICE as premature.
