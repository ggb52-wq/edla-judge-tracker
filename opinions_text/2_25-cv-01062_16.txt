The Court, having considered the Complaint, the record, the applicable law,
the Report and Recommendation of Partial Dismissal of the United States Magistrate

Report and Recommendation of Partial Dismissal of the United States Magistrate
Judge and adopts it as its opinion in this matter.1 Therefore,

defendants the Orleans Parish Sheriff’s Office, Orleans Justice Center, the Medical
Department, Deputy Rodriguez, SRT Officer Harris, and SRT Officer Patton, Sheriff
