ORDER
The Court, having considered the motion, the record, the applicable law, the Magistrate
Judge’s Report and Recommendation  (Rec. doc. 17), and the failure of Plaintiff to file
any objections to the Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation, hereby approves the
Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation and adopts it as its opinion herein. Accordingly;
IT IS ORDERED that Plaintiff’s claims under 42 U.S.C.  § 1983 against  Defendant
Louisiana State are DISMISSED WITH PREJUDICE as frivolous and otherwise for failure to
state a claim for which relief can be granted under 28 U.S.C. § 1915A.
New Orleans, Louisiana, this 5th day of October, 2023.
