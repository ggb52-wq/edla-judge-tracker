SECTION “B” (2)

 ORDER

The Court, having reviewed the complaint, the applicable law, and the Magistrate Judge’s
Report and Recommendation, and finding that Defendant’s Motion to Reverse and Remand was
unopposed and as of this date Plaintiff has filed no objection to the  Magistrate Judge’s Report and
Recommendation, approves the Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation and adopts it as
its opinion herein.
 Accordingly, IT IS ORDERED  that Defendant’s Unopposed Motion to Reverse and
Remand (ECF No. 15) is GRANTED, and judgment is entered R EVERSING the
Commissioner’s decision and REMANDING the case for further administrative proceedings.
 IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that Plaintiff’s Motion for Summary Judgment (ECF No.
12) is DENIED AS MOOT.
