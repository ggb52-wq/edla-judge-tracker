ORDER
 Having considered the complaint, the record, the applicable law, the magistrate judge’s
Report and Recommendation (“R&R”),1 and the failure of any party to file an objection to the
R&R, the Court hereby approves the R&R and adopts it as its opinion in this matter.  Therefore,
 IT IS ORDERED that Thomas James Hunter’s petition for issuance of a writ of habeas
corpus filed pursuant to 28 U.S.C. § 2254 is DISMISSED WITH PREJUDICE as time-barred.
