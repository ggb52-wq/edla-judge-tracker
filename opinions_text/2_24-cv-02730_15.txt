 The Court, having considered the Complaint,1 the record, the applicable law, the Report
and Recommendation of the U nited States Magistrate Judge, 2 and Plaintiff’s response  to the

and Recommendation and adopts it as its opinion in this matter.  Accordingly,
 IT IS ORDERED that Plaintiff’s Complaint is DISMISSED WITHOUT PREJUDICE
for lack of subject-matter jurisdiction under 28 U.S.C. § 1915(e)(2)(B).
