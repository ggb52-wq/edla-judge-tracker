REPORT AND RECOMMENDATION
In August 2022, pro se petitioner Keith Kirk filed the above-captioned application for
federal habeas corpus relief .1   Upon receipt  of the application, the Clerk’s Office issued a
standard deficiency notice to Kirk warning that his application was deficient for failure to
submit the proper form petition and include eith er an application to proceed in forma
pauperis or the requisite $5.00 filing fee.  Kirk failed to comply with  the deficiency notice. 2
The undersigned issued an order providing Kirk another opportunity to comply, but he failed
to do so. 3  To date, the above-captioned federal habeas application remains deficient.  See
Rules 2(c)-(d), 3(a) of the Rules Governing Section 2254 Cases in the United States District
Courts.  Accordingly, the instant application should be dismissed without prejudice for this
reason.  See e.g., Searls v. Cain, Civ. Action No. 08 -928, 2008 WL 1745142 (E.D. La. Apr. 10,
2008).4
