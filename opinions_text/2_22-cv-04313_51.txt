SECTION “G” (2)
ORDER AND REASONS

Defendant Illinois Union Insurance Company’s Motion to Compel Initial Disclosures (ECF
No. 49) is pending before me in this matter.  The motion was scheduled for submission on October
23, 2024.  As of this date, Plaintiff has not filed an Opposition Memorandum, and the deadline for
same expired on Tuesday, October 15, 2024.  See E.D. La. L.R. 7.5.
The Court’s February 7, 2024, Scheduling Order required the parties to exchange Rule
26(a)(1) initial disclosures within 7 days of the Rule 26(f) conference.1  Although movant delivered
initial disclosures (ECF No. 49-1 at 2), Plaintiff did not.  When the parties fail to hold a Rule 26(f)
conference, the court may deem discovery  to begin after the Rule 16 conference. 2  Accordingly,
considering the record, lack of opposition, applicable law, and finding the motion has merit,
IT IS ORDERED that the Motion to Compel Initial Disclosures is GRANTED.  Plaintiff
must provide its initial disclosures on or before October 30, 2024.
