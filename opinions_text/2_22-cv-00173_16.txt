ORDER AND REASONS
The Court, having considered de novo  the Petition for Writ of Habeas Corpus, 1 the
record, the applicable law, the Report and Recommendation of the United States Magistrate
Judge,2 and the failure of the Plaintiff to file any objection to the Magistrate Judge’s Report
and Recommendation,3 hereby approves the Report and Recommendation and adopts it as
its opinion in this matter. In doing so, the Court notes that it has construed Plaintiff Larry
Davis’s pro se pleadings liberally.4
 IT IS HEREBY ORDERED that Plaintiff’s 28 U.S.C. § 2254 5 claims alleged in the
Complaint6 are DISMISSED for the reasons stated in the Report and Recommendation and
Plaintiff’s claims against Harry Lee and Joseph Lopinto are DISMISSED WITHOUT
PREJUDICE.
 New Orleans, Louisiana, May 31, 2022.

WENDY B. VITTER
