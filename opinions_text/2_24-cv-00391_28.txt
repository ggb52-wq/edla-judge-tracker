ORDER
The Court, having considered the record, the applicable law, the Report and
Recommendation1 of the United States Magistrate Judge, and the failure 2 of the
Plaintiff to file an objection to the Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation,
hereby approves the Magistrate Judge ’s Report and Recommendation and adopts it
as its opinion in this matter.
Accordingly,
IT IS ORDERED  that Plaintiff’s premature 42 U.S.C. § 405(g) action be
STAYED and ADMINISTRATIVELY CLOSED pending the SSA’ s final decision
on its reconsideration of Plaintiff’s application for benefits, reserving to any party the
right to move to lift the stay within 60 days of issuance of that decision.
