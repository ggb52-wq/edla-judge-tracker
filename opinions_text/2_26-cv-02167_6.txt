ORDER

The Court, having considered the complaint, the record, the applicable law, the Report and
Recommendation of the United States Magistrate Judge, R. Doc. 4, and Plaintiff’s objection to the
Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation, R. Doc. 5, which the Court has reviewed de
novo, hereby approves the Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation and adopts it as its
opinion herein. Plaintiff’s allegations in his objection that the defendants individually knew about
the water leak and did nothing do not change the Court’s reasoning and determination that he has
failed to state a claim for relief. Further more, the Court finds that leave to amend his pleadings
would be futile despite the allegations in his objection. Therefore,
IT IS ORDERED that Ernest Montgomery’s complaint is  DISMISSED WITH
PREJUDICE as frivolous and/or for failure to state a claim on which relief may be granted.
