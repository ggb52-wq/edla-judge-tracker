ORDER

Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation and adopts it as its opinion in this matter.

IT IS ORDERED that Defendant’s Motion to Dismiss (R. Doc. 7) is GRANTED, and
Plaintiff’s claims are DISMISSED WITH PREJUDICE.
