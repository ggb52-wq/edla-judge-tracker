LAW AND ANALYSIS
I. Plaintiff has stated a plausible negligence claim against AGP.

 Plaintiff’s allegations state a plausible ne gligence claim against AGP. This Court
must determine AGP’s liability under Loui siana’s traditional duty-risk analysis.
Accordingly, to state a plausible claim of ne gligence, Plaintiff must allege facts that,
accepted as true, show that (1) he suffered an injury; (2) AGP owed him a duty of care; (3)
AGP breached that duty; (4) the conduct in question was the cause-in-fact of the resulting
harm; and (5) the risk of harm was within th e scope of protection afforded by the duty
