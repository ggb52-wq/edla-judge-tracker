ORDER A ND REASONS

Peter A. Grandpre, proceeding pro se, filed this lawsuit on June 23, 2022. 1
Grandpre, who has been incarcerated for the duration of the litigation, brought
numerous claims. The only ones that remain are for excessive force under § 1983.2
 The parties consented to proceed before a U.S. Magistrate Judge. On April 17,
2025, attorney Timothy B. Holden enrolled as counsel of record for Grandpre. 3 On
May 22, 2025, the Court issued a Scheduling Order setting a pretrial conference for
February 9, 2026, and a jury trial for March 9, 2026.4 On November 14, 2025, a
telephone status conference was held to discuss issues related to trial. 5 During the
status conference, the Court granted Holden’s request for a one-week continuance of
the trial date to allow additional time for trial preparation.6
 On January 26, 2026, a Motion to Withdraw Timothy B. Holden as Attorney
was filed. 7 The motion violated multiple requirements of the Local Rules and
