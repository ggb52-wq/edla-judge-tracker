O R D E R
The Court, having considered the complaint, the record, the applicable law, the United
States Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation, and the failure of any pa rty to file an
objection to the Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation, hereby approves the Magistrate
Judge’s Report and Recommendation and adopts it as its opinion in this matter.  Therefore,
IT IS ORDERED  that Justin Gallow’s petition for issuance of a writ of habeas corpus
filed pursuant to 28 U.S.C. § 2254 is DISMISSED WITHOUT PREJUDICE for improper venue
and lack of subject matter jurisdiction.
