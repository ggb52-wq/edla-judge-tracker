After considering the Motion and the applicable law, for the reasons expressed

The Court has reviewed and analyzed the four-factor test set forth above, and
finds that LIGA  satisfies a majority of the factors and may intervene as of right in
this case under Rule 24(a)(2).   As such, the Motion is granted and LIGA shall be
allowed to intervene as a defendant in this matter.
