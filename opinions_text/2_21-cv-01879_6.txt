ORDER
 Plaintiff’s complaint alleging claims under 42 U.S.C. § 1983 was
referred to Magistrate Judge Karen Wells Roby  for a Report and
Recommendation (“R&R”) and to conduct an evidentiary hearing if
necessary.  Magistrate Judge Roby subsequently recommended dismissal of
the complaint pursuant to Fed. R. Civ. P. 41(b) for plaintiff’s failure to
prosecute.1  Plaintiff did not object to the R&R.  Therefore, this Court reviews
the R&R for clear error.  See Douglass v. United Servs. Auto. Ass’n, 79 F.3d
1415, 1430 (5th Cir. 1996) (en banc) , superseded by statute on other
grounds, 28 U.S.C. § 636(b)(1) ; see also  Fed. R. Civ. P. 72(b) advisory
committee’s note (1983) (“When no timely objection is filed, the court need
only satisfy itself that there is no clear error on the face of the record in order
to accept the recommendation.”).  The Court finds no clear error.  Therefore,
the Court adopts Magistrate Judge Roby’s R&R as its opinion.
