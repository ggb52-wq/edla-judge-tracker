Prescription begins to run “from the time there is notice enough to call for
inquiry about a claim, not from the time when the inquiry reveals facts or evidence
sufficient to prove the claim.”39 It is uncontested that Plaintiffs received a statement
from Gulf Coast Bank in either December 2020 or January 2021 addressed to the
LLC, which caused Plaintiff My Tran alarm.40 Plaintiff testified that she made  an
inquiry by confronting her husband and thereafter sending a picture of the statement
to the Mortgage Broker.41 When asked to explain her concern, she testified that was
upset about receiving the statement addressed to “My Tung, LLC.” 42 Plaintiffs had
constructive notice in 2020 or 2021, and that notice was, in fact, “enough to call for
inquiry.”43 The Plaintiffs’ subjective understanding of an LLC is not relevant in
determining whether they had constructive notice to begin the prescriptive period.44
While the Court has the authority to reconsider and reverse its prior decision
under Rule 54 “for any reason it deems sufficient, even in the absence of new evidence
or an intervening change in or clarification of the substantive law,”45 the Court finds
no sufficient reason exists to do so on this motion.
