The Court, having considered the record, the applicable law, the Report
and Recommendation of the United States Magistrate Judge, and the failure of the plaintiff to
file an objection to the Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation, hereby approves the
Report and Recommendation of the United States Magistrate Judge and adopts it as its opinion in
this matter.  Therefore,
IT IS ORDERED  that Torrance Clark’s 42 U.S.C.  ' 1983 claim  against the Jefferson
Parish Sheriff’s Office and Jefferson Parish Correctional  Center  are DISMISSED  WITH
PREJUDICE as frivolous and for failure to state a claim for which relief can be granted pursuant
to 28 U.S.C. ' 1915(e), ' 1915A, and 42 U.S.C. ' 1997e.
