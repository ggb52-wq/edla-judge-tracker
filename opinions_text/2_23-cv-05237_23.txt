A.  The LPLA Claim
 1.  Applicable Law

“establishes the exclusive theories of liability for manufacturers for damage caused by their
products.”98 Louisiana Revised Statue §  9:2800.53(1) defines a “manufacturer” as “a person or

refurbishing a product’ as opposed to merely cosmetic repairs[]” or influencing the product in a

of products not falling under the LPLA’s definition of ‘manufacturer.’”102

 Based on the foregoing, the Court finds that Zi mmer Biomet have not carried their “heavy
