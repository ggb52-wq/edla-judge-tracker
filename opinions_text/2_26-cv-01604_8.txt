ORDER AND REASONS
 Plaintiff Barbara J. Gordon filed this pro se  and in forma pauperis
complaint asserting claims under 42 U.S.C. § 1983 against twenty-four
defendants in their individual and official capacities. 1  Plaintiff’s suit arises
out of a violent encounter that resulted  in plaintiff’s conviction for battery
and the loss of plaintiff’s nursing license. 2  On September 14, 2026,
Magistrate Judge Dossier issued a Report and Recommendation (“R&R”)
recommending that the Court dismiss plaintiff’s complaint without prejudice
as legally frivolous under 28 U.S.C. § 1915(e)(2). 3  Plaintiff did not object to
the R&R.  Therefore, the Court reviews the R&R for clear error.  See Douglass
v. United Servs. Auto. Ass’n, 79 F.3d 1415, 1430 (5th Cir. 1996) (en banc),
superseded by statute on other grounds, 28 U.S.C. § 636(b)(1); see also Fed.
R. Civ. P. 72(b) advisory committee’s note (1983) (“When no timely objection
