ORDER
Having considered the complaint, the record, the applicable law, the Report and
Recommendation (“R&R”) of the United States Magistrate Judge ,1 and the plaintiff’s objections
to the R&R,2 the Court hereby approves the R&R and adopts it as its opinion in this matter.  In his
objections, plaintiff repeats several  of the arguments he raised in his petition for writ of habea s
corpus.3  Those arguments were thoroughly considered by the magistrate judge .4  In addition to
the reiterated argument s, plaintiff contends that the Magistrate Ju dge “ has simply adopted the
State’s arguments which mischaracterize and misconstrue the issues presented. ”5  The Court has
reviewed the record and the R&R, and agrees with the Magistrate Judge’s characterization of the
issues.
Accordingly, for the foregoing reasons,
 IT IS ORDERED that plaintiff Roy Dixon’s objection is OVERRULED.
IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that the petition of Roy Dixon for issuance of a writ of habeas
corpus under 28 U.S.C. § 2241 be DISMISSED WITH PREJUDICE.
