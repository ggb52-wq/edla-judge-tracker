the applicable law, the Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation (R. Doc. 20),

Recommendation and adopts it as its opinion herein.
Accordingly,
IT IS HEREBY ORDERED that the Motion to Dismiss ( R. Doc.  18) is
GRANTED, and Plaintiff’s Complaint is DISMISSED WITH PREJUDICE.
