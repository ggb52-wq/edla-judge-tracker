ORDER
The Court, having considered the complaint , the record, the applicable law, the
United States Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation, and the failure of any
party to file an objection to the Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation, hereby
approves the Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation and adopts it as its opinion
in this matter.
Accordingly;
IT IS ORDERED that Norman Pete’s petition for issuance of a writ of habeas
corpus filed pursuant to 28 U.S.C. § 2254 is DISMISSED WITHOUT PREJUDICE for
lack of jurisdiction and failure to establis h authorization to proceed with a second or
successive petition.
