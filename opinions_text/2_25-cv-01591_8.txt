ORDER

The Court, having considered the Complaint, the record, the applicable law, the Report and
Recommendation of the United States Magistrate Judge, Rec. Doc. 7, and the failure of any party
to file an objection to the Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation, hereby approves the
Report and Recommendation of the United States Magistrate Judge and adopts it as its opinion in
this matter. Therefore,
IT IS HEREBY ORDERED  that Collins’ federal civil rights claims against defendants
are DISMISSED WITH PREJUDICE.
It is that  FURTHER ORDERED that Collins’s state law claims are DISMISSED
WITHOUT PREJUDICE.
New Orleans, Louisiana, this 24th day of October, 2025.
