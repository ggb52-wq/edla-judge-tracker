ORDER AND REASONS
Before the Court is a Motion for Entry of Judgment filed by Plaintiffs Michael
J. Berry and Cheryl Berry. 1  The Court previously granted Plaintiffs’ Motion to
Enforce Compromise. 2 Because such relief constitutes  “other relief” not set out in
Fed. R. Civ. P. 58(b), Plaintiffs now move under Fed. R. Civ. P. 58(d) for Judgment to
be set out in a separate document.
The Fifth Circuit has held that once a court finds that the parties had entered
into an agreement to settle the case, “a federal court may hold them to their word by
incorporating the terms of their agreement into a final judgmen t.” 3  Having
previously found that the parties entered into a binding settlement agreement under
Louisiana law, 4 the Court now finds it appropriate to incorporate that agreement
into a Judgment.
Accordingly,
