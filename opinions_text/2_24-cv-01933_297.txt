ORDER AND REASONS
 Before the Court is Plaintiffs’ Motion for Curative Rule 56 Procedure, to Modify
Language Assistance Restriction, and to Preserve Objections to Compressed
Summary Judgment Practice. 1 The Defendants jointly  filed a  Response
Memorandum in Opposition to Plaintiffs’ Motion.2 After consideration of the parties’
memoranda, the record, and the applicable law, the Court DENIES Plaintiffs’ Motion
for Curative Rule 56 Procedure.
I. INTRODUCTION
 Plaintiffs’ Motion primarily centers on the deposition transcript of Mr. Vo. 3
Plaintiffs seek a Rule 30(e) review of Mr. Vo’s deposition transcript to review for
possible errors due to Mr. Vo’s medical fragility and limited English proficiency. The
Plaintiffs argue that Defendants’ use of Mr. Vo’s deposition in their Motions for
Summary Judgment is improper without an additional opportunity to review the
transcript for inaccuracies. Plaintiffs’ Motion also argues that there are issues with
