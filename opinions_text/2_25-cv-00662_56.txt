ORDER AND REASONS
Before the Court is pro se Plaintiff Randy Martono-Chai’s second Motion
to Extend Time to Complete Service u nder Federal Rule of Civil Procedure
4(m) (Doc. 53). For the following reasons, Plaintiff’s Motion is GRANTED.

BACKGROUND
 On April 5, 2025, Plaintiff filed this action against Defendants Empower
Consulting Group LLC; Onyx Ecom, LLC; Limitless Profits LLC; Eyad Abbas;
Jason Rey Rodriguez; Andrew Giorgi; Blake Evertsen; and Kevin Vieira.
Plaintiff alleges that Defendants engaged in racketeering, fraud, and unfair
trade practices, committed various state law torts, and breached contracts with
Plaintiff. After failing to serve Defend ants Giorgi, Evertsen, and Viera, the
Plaintiff filed his first Motion to Extend Time to Complete Service. 1 On May
15, 2026, the Court granted Plaintiff’s Motion after finding good cause and
gave Plaintiff an additional 90 days to serve Defendants.2 Despite attempts by
the U.S. Marshals Service, Defendants Giorgi, Evertsen, and Vieira remain

LAW AND ANALYSIS
Plaintiff asks the Court for an additional 90 days to serve Defendants
Giorgi, Evertsen, and Vieira pursuant to Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 4(m).
Rule 4(m) commands the Court to grant an extension of the time limit to serve
defendants “if the plaintiff shows good cause for the failure.” Here, Plaintiff is
proceeding pro se  and has unsuccessfully attempted to serve Defendants
Giorgi, Evertsen, and Vieira via the U.S. Marshals Service  during his
extension. Plaintiff obtained a new address for Defendant Evertsen and
submitted materials to the U.S. Marshals Service to ef fect service. This
attempt failed; however, Plaintiff is continuing his attempt to locate an address
at which Defendant Evertsen can be served. In addition, service upon
Defendants Vieira and  Giorgi is ongoing. Because Plaintiff appears to have
made diligent efforts to serve each Defendant, the Court finds that good cause
exists to grant Plaintiff an extension of time to serve the unserved Defendants.

CONCLUSION
For the foregoing reasons,
IT IS ORDERED that Plaintiff’s Motion is GRANTED. Plaintiff shall
have an additional 90 days from entry of this Order to serve Defendants Giorgi,
Evertsen, and Vieira.
