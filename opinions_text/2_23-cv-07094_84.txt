O R D E R

 The Court, having considered the complaint, the record, the applicable law, the Report and
Recommendation of the United States Magistrate Judge, R. Doc. 81, and the failure of any party
to file an objection to the Magistrate Judge’ s Report and Recommendation, hereby approves the
Report and Recommendation of the United States Magistrate Judge and adopts it as its opinion in
this matter.  Therefore,
IT IS ORDERED that the Joint Motion to Stay Proceedings and for Extension of Pretrial
Deadlines on Behalf of Defendants Brad Cheramie, Robert Stolz, and Todd Rivere, R. Doc. 76, is
GRANTED.
It is FURTHER ORDERED that the claims against defendants Brad Cheramie, Robert
Stolz, and Todd Rivere are STAYED.
It is FURTHER ORDERED that the Clerk of Court mark this action CLOSED  for
statistical purposes. Within a reasonable time after the finality of Nicolais’s conviction or upon
disposition of the charges in his favor, plaintiff, or any other party, may move to reopen the matter
for further proceedings consistent with Heck v. Humphrey, 512 U.S. 477 (1994). The failure to
timey file a motion to reopen could waive his opportunity to proceed with this civil action.
