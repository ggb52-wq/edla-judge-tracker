As far as the Events that occurred more than 45 days before December 6, 2021 (Events 2-
6), in National R.R. Passenger Corp. v. Morgan, the Supreme Court held that “[b]ecause [a hostile
work environment claim] is composed of a series of separate acts that collectively constitute
‘unlawful employment practice,’ it does not matter that some of the component acts fall outside
the statutory time period.”27 The Court further held t hat “a charge may be filed at a later date and
still encompass the whole.” 28 Thus, even though Events 2-6 occurred more than 45 days before
Plaintiff contacted the EEO, they may be considered as a part of the hostile work environment as
a whole. It is for the jury to determine if these events, when considered together, show that that
Plaintiff was subject to a hostile work environment based on her race, religion, and national origin.
