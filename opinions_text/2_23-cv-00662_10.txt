ORDER
  The Court, having considered the record, applicable law, the Report and Recommendation
of the United States Magistrate Judge (R. Doc. 9), and the failure of the plaintiff to file an objection
to the Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation, hereby approves the Rep ort and
Recommendation of the United States Magistrate Judge and adopts it as its opinion in this matter.
Accordingly,
IT IS ORDERED that Ryan Charles Hurst’s § 1983 claims against Defendant Tanner
Cody Hurst are DISMISSED WITH PREJUDICE pursuant to 28 U.S.C. § 1915 and §1915A as
frivolous and for failure to state claim for which relief can be granted.
New Orleans, Louisiana, this 3rd day of August, 2023.
