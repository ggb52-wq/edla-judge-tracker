In Swales v. KLLM Transport Services, L.L.C. , the Fifth Circuit rejected the

case, what facts and legal considerations will be material to determining whether a group

In their reply memorandum, the Defendants advance the unsupported proposition
that “[a]t the decertification stage, even post-Swales, it is still Plaintiffs’ burden to prove
