I. BACKGROUND
This case arises out of an alleged contractual dispute concerning the
replacement of Dry Dock No. 3 at Joint Base Pearl Harbor-Hick in Hawaii.5  Plaintiff
LAD is a Louisiana limited liability company that specializes in dry dock fabrication,
new barge fabrication, structural fabrication, dock side repair, and marine salvage. 6
Lee Dragna (“Dragna”), the sole member of LAD, serves as LAD’s president and
owner.7  Defendant DHO is a general partnership organized in Hawaii that routinely
bids on shipyard infrastructure work provided by the Naval Facilities Engineering
Systems Command Pacific.8
DHO was awarded with a $2.8 billion contract from the United States Navy
for the construction and replacement of Dry Dock No. 3 with Dry Dock No. 5 at Joint
Base Pearl Harbor-Hickam (“the Dry Dock No. 5 project”).9  Over a period of several
months, LAD and DHO engaged in negotiations regarding the use of LAD as
subcontractor to construct a dry dock to be used during the Dry Dock No. 5 project.10
The negotiations indicate that if LAD was selected a s a subcontractor, LAD would
construct the dry dock in Louisiana , and then the dry dock would be transported to
Hawaii for use in the Dry Dock No. 5 project.11  The details of these negotiations are
discussed further in the Court’s August 5, 2026 Order and Reasons.12
