important factors” considered when determining the persuasiveness of a medical source’s opinions
or prior administrative medical findings.42
The supportability factor hinges on relevance; “the more relevant the objective medical
evidence and supporting explanations presented by a medical source are to support his or her
medical opinion(s) or prior administrative medical finding(s), the more persuasive the [opinion or
finding] will be.”43  Similarly, when assessing consistency, which is the other “most important”
factor, “the more consistent a medical opinion(s) or prior administrative medical finding(s) is with
the evidence from other medical sources and nonmedical sources in the claim, the more persuasive
the [opinion or finding] will be.”44
