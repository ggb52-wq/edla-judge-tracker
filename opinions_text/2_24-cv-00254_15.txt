ORDER
The Court, having considered the complaint, the record, the applicable law, the Report and
Recommendation of the United States Magistrate Judge, and the failure of any party to file an
objection to the Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation, hereby approves the Magistrate
Judge’s Report and Recommendation and adopts it as its opinion in this matter.  Therefore,
IT IS ORDERED  that Kevin Robert Sterling ’s 42 U.S.C. § 1983 c omplaint against
defendants Warden Denise Narcisse, the Plaquemines Parish Sheriff, and the Plaquemines Parish
Sheriff’s Office be DISMISSED WITH PREJUDICE  pursuant to 28 U.S.C. §  1915(e) and
§ 1915A, and as applicable, 42 U.S.C. § 1997e, as frivolous and otherwise for failure to state a
claim for which relief can be granted.
