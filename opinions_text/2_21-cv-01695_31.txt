ORDER
 Plaintiff’s complaint alleging claims under 42 U.S.C. § 1983 was
referred to Magistrate Judge Donna Phillips Currault  for a Report and
Recommendation (“R&R”) and to conduct an evidentiary hearing if
necessary.  After plaintiff repeatedly failed to comply with the Court’s orders
and did not attend scheduled conferences , Magistrate Judge Currault
recommended dismissal of plaintiff’s claims for failure to prosecute .1  See
Fed. R. Civ. P. 41(b).  Plaintiff did not object to the R&R.  Therefore, this
Court reviews the R&R for clear error.  See Douglass v. United Servs. Auto.
Ass’n, 79 F.3d 1415, 1430 (5th Cir. 1996) (en banc), superseded by statute on
other grounds, 28 U.S.C. § 636(b)(1); see also Fed. R. Civ. P. 72(b) advisory
committee’s note (1983) (“When no timely objection is f iled, the court need
only satisfy itself that there is no clear error on the face of the record in order
