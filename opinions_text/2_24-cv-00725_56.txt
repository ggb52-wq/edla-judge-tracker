ORDER

The Court, after considering the defendant’s motion, the record, the applicable law,
the Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation,1 and finding that no party has filed
objections to the Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation, hereby approves the
Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendat ion and adopts it as its opinion.
Accordingly,
IT IS ORDERED that Defendant Darren Robin’s Motion to Dismiss, or
Alternatively, to Quash Return on Summons 2 be DENIED in part, as to the motion to
dismiss and GRANTED in part, as to the motion to quash the return of service and that
the summons executed on Defendant Darren Robin at 500 Poydras Street in New Orleans,
Louisiana3 be QUASHED for insufficient service.
New Orleans, Louisiana, this 3rd day of July, 2025.
