ORDER
On August 11, 2026, the Magistrate Judge issued a Report and Recommendation,
recommending this Court dismiss Plaintiff’s claims with prejudice as frivolous and
otherwise for failure to state a claim for which relief can be granted.1 A copy of the Report
and Recommendation was mailed to Plaintiff at his listed address. 2 However, on August
28, 2026, the mailed copy was returned to the Court as “undeliverable,” 3 meaning the
Plaintiff has changed addresses without informing the Court and he did not receive a copy
of the Report and Recommendation. Although Plaintiff has not objected to the Report and
Recommendation and the  Court finds that the Magistrate Judge’s Report and
Recommendation is not clearly erroneous or contrary to law, the Court will not dismiss
Plaintiff’s claims with prejudice without Plaintiff having received actual notice of the
Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recom mendation. As a result, the Court will  adopt the
Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation in part and dismiss Plaintiff’s claims
without prejudice rather than dismissing them with prejudice. Accordingly,
The Court, having considered the record, the applicable law, relevant filings, and
the Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation, ADOPTS the Magistrate Judge’s
