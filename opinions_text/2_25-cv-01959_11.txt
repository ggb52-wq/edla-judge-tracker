ORDER AND REASONS
Before the Court is pro se plaintiff Yu Hin Chan’s motion to proceed in
forma pauperis on appeal with the Fifth Circuit.1  For the reasons that follow,
the Court denies Chan’s motion.
A claimant may proceed with an appeal in forma pauperis if she meets
three requirements.  First, the claima nt must submit “an affidavit that
includes a statement . . . that [she] is unable to pay such fees or give security
