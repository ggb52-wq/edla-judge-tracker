 ORDER

 Having considered the complaint, the record, the applicable law, the magistrate judge’s
Report and Recommendation (“R&R”),1 and the failure of any party to file an objection to the
R&R, the Court hereby approves the R&R and adopts it as its opinion in this matter.  Accordingly,
IT IS ORDERED that plaintiff’s comp laint is DISMISSED WITHOUT PREJUDICE
because plaintiff failed to state a claim under 42 U.S.C. § 1983, thereby depriving the Court of
subject-matter jurisdiction over such claim an d any basis for supplemental jurisdiction over
plaintiff’s state-law claims.
