ORDER

 The Court, having considered the motion, the record, the applicable law, the
Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation  (Rec. Doc. 15), and the failure of Plaintiff
to file any objections to the Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation, hereby
approves the Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommen dation and adopts it as its opinion
herein.
 Accordingly,
 IT IS ORDERED  that Plaintiff’s claims under 42 U.S.C. § 1983 against the Jefferson
Parish Sheriff’s Office  are DISMISSED WITH PREJUDICE  as frivolous and otherwise for
failure to state a claim for which relief can be granted under 28 U.S.C. § 1915A.
