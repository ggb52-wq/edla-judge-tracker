ORDER AND REASONS

 Plaintiff Charles Touchet moves to strike 1 the summary judgment reply
brief of defendants Dorgelys Manzano Borrell and Alive Free
Transportation.2
 The district court must either give  the other party an opportunity to
respond or decline to rely on new arguments and evidence presented for the
first time in a reply brief.  Georgia Firefighters’ Pension Fund v. Anadarko
Petroleum Corp., 99 F.4th 770, 774 (5th Cir. 2024).
 Here, Touchet asserts correctly that  defendants’ reply brief presents
new evidence and arguments.  The rep ly brief includes an attachment A not
previously provided to the Court and relies on the newly presented
“independent evidence” in the attachment.3
