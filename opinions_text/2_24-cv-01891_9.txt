ORDER AND REASONS

 Plaintiff Kecite Renee Smith, a state pretrial detainee housed in the St.
Tammany Parish Jail in Covington, Louisiana, filed this pro se federal civil
action pursuant to 42 U.S.C. § 1983 on July 26, 2024. 1  O n  N o v em b e r  1 9,
2024, Magistrate Judge Eva J. Dossier issued a Report and
Recommendation (“R&R”), recommending that the Court dismiss the action
as “frivolous” and/or for failure to state a claim on which relief may be
granted.2  Smith did not object to the R&R.  Therefore, the Court reviews the
R&R for clear error.  See Douglass v. United Servs. Auto. Ass’n, 79 F.3d 1415,
1430 (5th Cir. 1996) (en banc), superseded by statute on other grounds , 28
U.S.C. § 636(b)(1); see also Fed. R. Civ. P. 72(b) advisory committee’s note
(1983) (“When no timely objection is filed, the court need only satisfy itself
