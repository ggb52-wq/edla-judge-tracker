THOAI NGUYEN, ET AL.  CIVIL ACTION

VERSUS  NO. 24-518

JP MORGAN CHASE, N.A.  SECTION A (2)

ORDER

The Court having conducted a de novo review of the portions of the magistrate
judge’s Report and Recommendation implicated by the plaintiffs’ objections, concludes
that none of the plaintiffs’ objections have merit. The Court therefore denies the
plaintiffs’ objections (Rec. Doc. 68), approves the Report and Recommendation of the
United States Magistrate Judge, and adopts it as its opinion in this matter.
Within 14 days from entry of this Order the plaintiffs shall execute the settlement
agreement in accordance with the magistrate judge’s Report and Recommendation.
Upon conclusion of that 14-day window, the Court will dismiss this matter with prejudice
pursuant to a final judgment after which jurisdiction in this Court will terminate and any
further challenges will lie with the court of appeals.
October 1, 2026
