ORDER
The Court, after considering the complaint , the record, the applicable law, the
Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation, and finding that as of this date the
Plaintiff has filed no objections to the Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation,1
hereby approves the Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation and adopts it as its
opinion.
Accordingly,
IT IS ORDERED that this lawsuit is DISMISSED for failure to state a claim and
for seeking monetary relief against Defend ants who are immune from suit under 28
U.S.C. § 1915(e)(2)(B)(ii-iii).
 New Orleans, Louisiana, this 5th day of January, 2026.

SUSIE MORGAN
UNITED STATES DISTRICT JUDGE
