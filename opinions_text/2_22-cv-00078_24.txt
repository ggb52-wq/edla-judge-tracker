O R D E R
The Court, having considered the complaint, the record, the applicable law, the Report and
Recommendation of the United States Magistrate Judge, and the objection to the Magistrate
Judge’s Report and Recommendation, hereby approves the Magistrate Judge’s Report and
Recommendation and adopts it as its opinion in this matter.  Therefore,
IT IS ORD ERED that plaintiff Justin Antonio Hutchinson’s 42 U.S.C. §  1983 claims
against defendants St. Tammany Parish Sheriff Randy Smith, St. Tammany Parish District
Attorney Warren Montgomery, St. Tammany Parish Jail Warden Daniel Fleischman, Dr. Jose
Ham, and Dr. Samuel Gore are DISMISSED WITH PREJUDICE  pursuant to 28 U.S.C.
§ 1915(e), § 1915A, and as applicable, 42 U.S.C. § 1997e, as frivolous, for failure to state a claim
for which relief can be granted, and as to defendant Montgomery, for seeking relief against an
immune defendant.
IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that Hutchinson’s state law claims of slander, defamation,
malicious prosecution, negligence, and malpractice are DISMISSED WITHOUT PREJUDICE
because the court declines to exercise supplemental jurisdiction under 28 U.S.C. § 1367(c).
