Plaintiff has not established that reconsideration is appropriate as  to Defendant Lamb.  Plaintiff
was notified that this case would be dismissed for failure to properly serve the defendants within
the specified time period and the Court explicitly addressed the requirements for proper service.
Rather than properly serve the defendants, Plaintiff directed the U.S. Marshal Service to serve a
summon on Lamb using an “and/or ” designation for delivery at Am azon’s Seattle headquarters.
