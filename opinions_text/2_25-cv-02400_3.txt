ORDER & REASONS
 Plaintiff Alik Skripnikov (“plaintiff”) filed a habeas corpus petition1 pursuant
to 28 U.S.C. § 2241, challenging the validity of his detention in Richwood Correctional
Center on immigration charges. Before considering the merits of plaintiff’s petition,
“the Court must determine as a threshold matter whether it has proper jurisdiction
to entertain [p]laintiff ’s habeas petition pu rsuant to 28 U.S.C. § 2241(a). ” Dada v.
Witte, No. 20 -1093, 2020 WL 1674129, at *2 ( E.D. L a. Apr. 6, 2020).  “ [T]he only
district that may consider a habeas corpus challenge to present physical confinement
pursuant to § 2241 is the district court in which the prisoner is confined.” Id. (quoting
United States v. McPhearson , 451 F.App ’x 384, 387 (5th Cir. 2011)) ; 28 U.S.C.
§ 2241(d) ( “. . . the application may be filed in the district  court for the district
wherein such person is in custody . . . .”).
 Richwood Correctional Center is located in Monroe, Louisiana. 2 Monroe,
