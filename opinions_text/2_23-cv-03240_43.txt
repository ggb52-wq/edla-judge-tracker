for damage to the property. 4 Splendid Dwellings alleges th e payments it received from
Underwriters were inadequate to cover the costs to repair the damages the property
suffered from Hurricane Ida.5

Law and Analysis

just orders . . . if a party or its attorney . . . fails to obey a scheduling or other pretrial
order.” Trial courts, however, have “broad discretion to preserve the integrity and
purpose” of a scheduling order.15 When determining whether to exclude expert testimony
as improperly designated, district courts in the Fifth Circuit consider four factors: “(1) the
