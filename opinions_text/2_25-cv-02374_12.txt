ORDER

The Court, having considered the Complaint, the record, the applicable
law, the Report and Recommendation of the United States Magistrate Judge,
and the Supplemental Report and Recommendation, hereby approves the
Report and Recommendation of the United States Magistrate Judge, as
supplemented, and adopts it as its opinion in this matter.1
Therefore,
IT IS THEREFORE ORDERED that Charley James’s federal civil
rights claims against defendants Carolyn Thomas and Mrs. Ellington are
DISMISSED pursuant to 28 U.S.C. § 1915, § 1915(e), § 1915A, and 42 U.S.C.
§ 1997e as frivolous and otherwise for failure to state a claim for which relief
can be granted.
