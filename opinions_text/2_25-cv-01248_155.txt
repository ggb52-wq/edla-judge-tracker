ORDER & REASONS
Before the Court is a Motion to Strike Witness and Exhibit Lists filed by Plaintiff Kathy
Sherrod. R. Doc. 107. Therein, Plaintiff urges the Court to strike Defendants’ witness and exhibit
list as it was filed seventeen days after the Court’s deadline. Id. Defendants Nexion Health
Management, Inc . and Nexion Health at Marrero, Inc. d/b/a Marrero Healthcare Cente r
(“Defendants”) oppose the Motion. R. Doc. 140. In opposition, Defendants take responsibility for
the missed deadline, but maintain that the delay was largely inconsequential. To this end,
Defendants highlight th e substantial overlap between the parties’ witnesses and exhibits, the
considerable gap of time between Defendants’ late filing and the trial date, and the absence of an
intentional delay on the Defendants’ part, coupled with the fact that Defendants have no pattern of
habitual late filings. Id. at 3–6.
Under Fed. R. Civ. P. 26(a), litigants must produce to the other parties and promptly file
with the court information pertaining to the identity of each witness they intend to or may call and
identify all exhibits they intend to introduce. “If a party fails to provide information or identify a
witness as required by Rule 26(a) or (e), the party is not allowed to use that information or witness
to supply evidence on a motion, at a hearing, or at a trial, unless the failure was substantially
justified or is harmless.” Fed. R. Civ. P. 37(c)(1).

The Court agrees with Defendants that the late filing was harmless under the
circumstances. Defendants’ lists, while seventeen days overdue, were filed more than two-and-a-
half months before trial . Every witness, except one, listed on Defendants’ witness list was also
included on Plaintiff ’s witness list , and the single witness exclusive to Defendants ’ list had
previously produced an expert report and been deposed by Plaintiff.  Plaintiff, in her Motion,
demonstrated no prejudice that would result from the late filing. And the mistake, although clearly
the result of defense counsel’s error, was an isolated occurrence and not part of a broader pattern
of late filings. Accordingly;
IT IS ORDERED that Plaintiff’s Motion to Strike Witness and Exhibit Lists, R. Doc. 107,
is hereby DENIED.
