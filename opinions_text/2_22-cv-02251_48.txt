A Rule 59(e) motion is “not the vehicle for rehashing evidence, legal theories, or

Rule 59(e) “serve[s] the narrow purpose of allowing a party to correct manifest errors of
law or to present newly discovered evidence.” 33 “A manifest error is not shown by the
disappointment of the losing party, rather it  is the wholesale disregard, misapplication,
or failure to recognize controlling precedent.”34
