failure of any party to file an objection to the  Magistrate  Judge’s

Report and Recommendation and adopts it as its opinion in this matter.
Therefore,
IT IS ORDERED that Plaintiff Chad Steven Coco’s §1983 claims against
defendants Lafourche Parish Sheriff’s Office and Unidentified Parties
be DISMISSED WITH PREJU DICE as frivolous and as otherwise failing to
state a claim for which relief can be granted pursuant to 28 U.S.C. § 1915, § 1915A
and 42 U.S.C. § 1997e.
