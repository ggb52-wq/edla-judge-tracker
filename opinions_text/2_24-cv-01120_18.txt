ORDER
The Court, having considered the complaint, the record, the applicable law, the Report and
Recommendation of the United States Magistrate Judge (R. Doc. 17), and the failure of any party
to file an objection to the Magistrate Judge ’s Report and Recommendation, hereby approves the
Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation and adopts it as its opinion in this matter.
Accordingly;
IT IS ORDERED that plaintiff Don Lewis’s  42 U.S.C. § 1983 complaint against
defendants, Captain/Warden Greg Baker and Warden Jonathan Griffin, is DISMISSED WITH
PREJUDICE for failure to prosecute. See Fed. R. Civ. P. 41(b).
New Orleans, Louisiana, this 20th day of October, 2025.
