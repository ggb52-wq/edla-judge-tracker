ORDER
Considering pro se petitioner Brian K. Banks’ motion to amend
judgment (Rec. Doc. 37),
IT IS HEREBY ORDERED that the motion is DENIED pursuant to
the Court’s order at Rec. Doc. 41 and the reasons set forth in the
Court’s order at Rec. Doc. 35.
Petitioner is a state court prisoner seeking release from
custody imposed in a state prison sentence. He mistakenly asserts
“His only remedy is this mandamus petition.” Rec. Doc. 34 at 12.
The constitutional grounds supportive of that petition and the
requested relief shall be properly addressed and construed in the
context of an application for a writ of habeas corpus pursuant to
congressional enactment found in 28 U.S.C. § 2254, not a mandamus
action.
