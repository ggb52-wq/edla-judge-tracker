UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT
EASTERN DISTRICT OF LOUISIANA

ORDER & REASONS
Plaintiff Paul Hayes filed this action nami ng the St. James Parish Sheriff’s Office
(“SJPSO”), among others, as a defendant.1  The SJPSO filed a motion to dismiss, arguing that it is
not a legal entity capable of being sued. 2  Thereafter, Hayes filed an amended complaint that
removed the SJPSO as a defendant. 3  Because the operative complaint removes the SJPSO as a
defendant to this action,
IT IS ORDERED that the SJPSO’s motion to  dismiss (R. Doc. 16)  is DISMISSED AS
MOOT, and Hayes’s claims against the SJPSO are DISMISSED WITHOUT PREJUDICE.
New Orleans, Louisiana, this 4th day of November, 2024.
