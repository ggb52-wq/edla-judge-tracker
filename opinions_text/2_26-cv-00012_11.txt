ORDER AND REASONS

 Plaintiff Claude Falcon moves for leave to file a second amended
complaint.1  Under Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 15(a)(2), the court grants
leave to amend “when justice so requires.”  Here, plaintiff has acted in good
faith and quickly; the original compla int was filed fewer than twenty-one
days ago.  The amendment will not cause any prejudice to defendant.
For the foregoing reasons, the Court GRANTS Falcon’s motion for
leave to file.  The Court orders the proposed amended complaint entered into
the record as plaintiff’s second amended complaint.
New Orleans, Louisiana, this 14th day of January, 2026.
