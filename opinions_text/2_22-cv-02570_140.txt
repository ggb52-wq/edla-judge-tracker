an unjust advantage for one party or to cause a loss or inconvenience to another,” and “may also
result from silence or inaction.”52  A plaintiff bringing a claim for fraud under Louisiana law must
prove the following elements: (1) a misstatement or omission (2) of a material fact (3) made with
the intent to defraud (4) on which the plaintiff relied and (5) which proximately caused the
plaintiff’s injury.53  Under Louisiana law, “[f]raud cannot be predicated on unfulfilled promises or
