ORDER
The Court,  after  considering  the  complaint,  the  record,  the  applicable  law,  the
Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation, and finding that as of this date the
Plaintiff has filed no objections to the Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation,
hereby approves the Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation and adopts it as its
opinion.  Accordingly,
IT IS ORDERED that the Motion for Default Judgment1 is DENIED
and defendant Hilton New Orleans Riverside is DISMISSED.
New Orleans, Louisiana, this 5th day of December, 2025.
