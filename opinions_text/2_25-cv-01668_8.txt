ORDER
The Court, having considered the reco rd, the applicable law, the Report and
Recommendation1 of the United States Magistrate Judge, and the failure 2 of the
Plaintiff Timothy Oneal Showers to file an objection to the Magistrate Judge’s Report
and Recommendation, hereby approves the Report and Recommendation of the
Magistrate Judge and adopts it as its opinion in this matter.
Accordingly,
IT IS ORDERED that Plaintiff’s complaint be DISMISSED WITHOUT
PREJUDICE for lack of subject matter jurisdiction.
