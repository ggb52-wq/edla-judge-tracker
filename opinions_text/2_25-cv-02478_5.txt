ORDER AND REASONS
Plaintiff Rico Newman (“Newman”) filed a Motion to Appoint Counsel (ECF No. 4) to
assist him in this  in forma pauperis  civil rights action under 42 U.S.C. § 1983, in which he
challenges the conditions of his confinement . Newman states that he  is unable to afford counsel
and has made efforts to obtain counsel but was declined. ECF No. 4 at 1-4.
Section 3(d) of the April 22, 2014 Resolution of the En Banc Court (permanently adopted
on October 5, 2016) provides: “In cases filed by prisoners, counsel may not be appointed from the
Panel until the Magistrate Judge has determined that the case should proceed beyond the screening
process required in 28 U.S.C. § 1915A.” The court is currently conducting its statutory
frivolousness review of Newman’s complaint pursuant to 28 U.S.C. § 1915A, § 1915(e)(2), and
42 U.S.C. § 1997e(c), as applicable. As part of that review, the court also may schedule a Spears1
hearing to further develop the facts alleged in the complaint. Only after completion of the required
screening process will this c ourt be in a position to assess whether “exceptional circumstances” 2
