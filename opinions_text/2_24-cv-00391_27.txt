REPORT AND RECOMMENDATION
Pro se Plaintiff S.W. applied for benefits on behalf of her minor child, B.W., in
connection with the death of the child’s alleged father. 1 She also moves the Court to
appoint pro bono counsel to represent her in this matter  (R. Doc. 24). Although
benefits were initially denied on the basis that Plaintiff had not established paternity,
Plaintiff has obtained new evidence, including a Judgment of Paternity2 and a Birth
Certificate3 identifying the decedent as B .W.’s father and  bolstering the evidence
previously presented to the Administrative Law Judge (“ALJ”).
The record indicates that the Appeals Council dismissed as untimely Plaintiff’s
request for review but referred this new evidence to the A LJ “to determine whether
it warrants any change in the decision.” 4 The Court ordered Defendant, the Social
Security Administration (“SSA”), to file a memorandum explaining the status of that
referral and any effect on the instant proceedings.5 In its response, the SSA indicates

be STAYED and ADMINISTRATIVELY CLOSED pending the SSA’s final decision
on its reconsideration of Plaintiff’s application for benefits, reserving to any party the
right to move to lift the stay within 60 days of issuance of that decision.
IT IS FURTHER RECOMMENDED that Plaintiff’s Motion for the
Appointment of Counsel (R. Doc. 24) be HELD IN ABEYANCE, without prejudice
to Plaintiff’s right to reassert her request for  pro bono counsel, if necessary, should
this matter ultimately be reopened.
