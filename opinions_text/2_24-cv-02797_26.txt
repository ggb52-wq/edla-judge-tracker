In his motion, Mr. Washington has simply re -raised the same arguments presented in his

Mr. Washington has included a quote supposedly from In re Merlo, 646 B.R. 389, 391
(Bankr. E.D.N.Y. 2022), but upon review, the Court could not locate the sentence anywhere
in the opinion. The Court thus finds no basis for disturbing its prior order. Accordingly;
IT
