set for submission on August 11, 2022.2   Local Rule 7.5 of the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Louisiana requires that a memorandum in opposition to a motion be filed no
later than eight days before the noticed submission date, which deadline in this instance was
August 3, 2022.  Plaintiffs Don and Penny Poole, on behalf of minor child G.C. (collectively,
“Plaintiffs”), who are represented by counsel, did not file an opposition.  Accordingly, because the
motion is unopposed and appears to have merit,3
