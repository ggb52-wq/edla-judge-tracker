The Court, after considering the complaint, the record, the applicable law, the Magistrate

objections to the Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation, hereby approves the Magistrate

IT IS ORDERED  that Oscar Dantzler’s claims ag ainst Claiborne W. Brown, the last
remaining Defendant in this matter, are DISMISSED WITHOUT PREJUDICE pursuant to FED.
R. CIV. P. 4(m) and FED. R. CIV. P. 41(b).
