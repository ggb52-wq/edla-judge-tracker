 The Court, after considering the complaint, the record, the applicable law, the Magistrate
Judge’s Report and Recommendations, 1 and finding that as of this  date plaintiff has filed no
objections to the Magistrate Judge’s Repor t and Recommendations, hereby approves the
Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendations and adopts it as its opinion.  Accordingly,
IT IS ORDERED that the Rule 12(b)(1) motion 2 to dismiss Plaintiff’s 42 U.S.C. § 1983
claims seeking monetary damages asserted agai nst Travis Day, Keith Bi ckham, Kevin Luper,
Karla Wheat, Dr. Juan Martinez, and Colone l Wade Rigdon in their of ficial capacity is
GRANTED and those claims are DISMISSED WITHOUT PREJUDICE, leaving for resolution
the §1983 claims against Defendants in their individual capacity.
IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that the Rule 12(b)(6) motion to dismiss Plaintiff’s ADA
claims asserted against Travis Day, Keith Bickham, Kevin Luper, Karla Wheat, Dr. Juan Martinez,
and Colonel Wade Rigdon in their individual capacity is GRANTED and those claims are
DISMISSED WITH PREJUDICE, leaving for resolution the ADA claims against Defendants in
their official capacity.
