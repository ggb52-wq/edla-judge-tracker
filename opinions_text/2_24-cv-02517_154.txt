 ORDER AND REASONS
Before the Court  are two Motions to Allow Late Filing of FLSA Consent to Join Forms
filed on behalf of Randy Vallian, Donald Arvie, Steven Fortenberry, Deshawn Tremond James,
and Leonard Banks. R. Docs. 152 and 153. The Motions were submitted to the Court on August
26, 2026, and September 9, 2026, respectively. Defendants have not filed an opposition to either
motion; accordingly, the Motions will be deemed to be unopposed.
The Fair Labor Standards Act (“FLSA”) provides for an opt -in procedure in a collective
action, but it does not specify when a person must opt -in or submit a written notice of consent to
join a case. See 29 U.S.C. §§ 216(b), 255, 256. The court instead sets the opt-in deadline. See id.
Because the statute should be read broadly and generously in favor of those it protects, courts have
discretion in matters pertaining to opt -in deadlines. See, e.g., Ferguson v. T exas Farm Bureau
Underwriters, Civ.A. 6-17-CV-00111-ADA, 2019 WL 6609636, at *2 (W.D. Tex. Dec. 5, 2019);
Coronado v. D.N.W. Houston, Inc., CIV.A. H-13-2179, 2014 WL 6674292, at *2 (S.D. Tex. Nov.
24, 2014) (observing the remedial purpose of the FLSA).

Courts usually balance several factors in determining whether an untimely consent to join
a collective action is allowed to stand: (1) whether “good cause” exists for the late submissions;
(2) prejudice to the defendant; (3) how long after the deadline passed the consent forms were filed;
(4) judicial economy; and (5) the remedial purposes of the FLSA. Coronado, 2014 WL 6674292,
at *2; see also Ruggles v. Wellpoint, Inc., 687 F. Supp. 2d 30, 37 (N.D.N.Y. 2009).
Here, the opt -in deadline was set for Jul y 27, 2026, and the consent forms  were signed
within a short period of time thereafter. Having reviewed the Motions and supporting documents,
the factors articulated in Ruggles, and the lack of formal opposition from Defendants, the Court
will grant the Motions.
Accordingly,
IT IS ORDERED that the Motions for Leave to File FLSA Consent  to Join Forms (R.
Docs. 152 and 153) are GRANTED.
