ORDER
The Court, after considering the complaint, the record, the applicable law, the
Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation (Rec. Doc. 28), and finding that as
of this date the parties have filed no objections to the Magistrate Judge’s Report and
Recommendation, hereby approves the Magistrate Judge’s Report and
Recommendation and adopts it as its opinion.  Accordingly,
 IT IS ORDERED  that the cross -motion of the defendant Kilolo Kijakazi,
Commissioner of the Social Security Administration, for summary judgment (Rec.
Doc. 26) is GRANTED; and (2) the motion of the plaintiff, Clyde J. Rixner, for
summary judgment (Rec. Doc. 24) is DENIED.
