Considering plaintiff Elbert Buckley’s motion for dismissal
of complaint certain claims without prejudice (Rec. Doc. 15) and
plaintiff’s counsel motion for dismissal of complaint (Rec. Doc.
14),
IT IS ORDERED that plaintiff Elbert Buckley’s motion for
dismissal of complaint certain claims without prejudice (Rec. Doc.
15) is GRANTED. Plaintiff asserts his right to dismiss without
prejudice pursuant to Federal Rules of Civil Procedure 41(a). The
provision provides for two possible grounds for dismissal without
prejudice: without a court order or by court order. The latter
ground permits the court to dismiss “on terms that the court
considers proper.” Fed. R. Civ. P. 41(a)(2). As the Fifth Circuit
has instructed, “The primary purpose in entrusting dismissal to
the supervision of the court under Rule 41(a)(2) is to protect the
non-movant from unfair treatment. Generally, courts approve such
dismissals unless the defendant will suffer some plain legal
prejudice other than the mere prospect of a second lawsuit.”
Ikospentakis v. Thalassic S.S. Agency, 915 F.2d 176, 177 (5th Cir.
