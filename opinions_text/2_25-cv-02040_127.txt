 ORDER

 On September 21, 2026, the United States Magistrate Judge issued a Report and
Recommendation (“R&R”) on the government’s motion for sanctions against defendant Unnisha
Paige arising from Paige’s viol ation of discovery orders. 1  The R&R recommended that the
government’s motion be granted and that the gover nment be awarded reasona ble attorney’s fees
in the amount of $1,536.00. 2  The R&R also recommended that  this Court issue an adverse
inference against Paige finding that Paige exercised oversight over Paige Tax franchisees, received
income from them, and participated in the prepar ation of tax returns for customers that included
false information.3  Objections to the R&R were due on or before October 5, 2026. 4  Paige did
not file an objection.  However, codefendant  Deshira J. Horton objected to the R&R and
“request[ed] an express limitation that any in ference based on Unnisha S. Paige’s discovery
violations applies so lely against Paige.” 5  Specifically, Horton asks the Court to sustain her
objection and include the follow ing limitation in any order a dopting the adverse-inference
recommendation:
Any adverse inference adopted from the [R&R] concerning Paige’s oversight of
Paige Tax franchisees, receipts of income from them, or participation in preparing
returns containing false information applie s solely against [d]efendant Unnisha S.

 The Court ordered the government to provide its position on Horton’s objection and her
requested modification to the ad verse-inference recommendation.7  The government responded
that it does not oppose the adverse inference being limited to Paige.8  But the government did not
address the specific language requested by Horton.9
Having considered the government’s motion for sanctions, the record, the applicable law,
the R&R, Horton’s objection to the R&R, and the government’s response, the Court hereby
approves the Report and Recommendation and adopts it as its opinion in this matter.  The Court
notes, though, that the adverse-inference recommendation is limited to Paige and adopts in whole
the limitation articulated in Horton’s objection as quoted above.  Therefore,
IT IS ORDERED that the government’s motion for sanctions against Paige (R. Doc. 107)
is GRANTED, and the government is awarded reasonable attorney ’s fees in the amount of
$1,536.00.
