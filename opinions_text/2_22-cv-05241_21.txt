ORDER

 Plaintiff Jade Ducre, proceeding pro se and in forma pauperis, brings
this action under 42 U.S.C. §  2000e, alleging discrimination on the basis of
race, color, and gender, as well as retaliation.1  On July 12, 2023, Magistrate
Judge Karen Wells Roby  issued a Report & Re commendation (“R&R”),
recommending that the Court dismiss plaintiff’s complaint pursuant to
Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 41(b) for her failure to prosecute.2
 Plaintiff did not object to the R&R.  Therefore, th e Court reviews the
R&R for clear error.  See Douglass v. United Servs. Auto. Ass’n, 79 F.3d 1415,
1430 (5th Cir. 1996) (en banc), superseded by statute on other grounds , 28
U.S.C. § 636(b)(1); see also Fed. R. Civ. P. 72(b) advisory committee’s note
(1983) (“When no timely objection is filed, the  court need only satisfy itself
