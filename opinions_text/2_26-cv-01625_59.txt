UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT
EASTERN DISTRICT OF LOUISIANA

ORDER & REASONS
Before the Court is a motion for partial summary judgment filed by defendant Am Purpose
Limited (“Am Purpose”) to dismiss the in rem claims against and Rule C arrest of the M/V Am
Purpose,1 to which plaintiff Ultrabulk Cargo Services GmbH responds in opposition.2  On August
11, 2026, this Court issued an Order & Reasons (R. Doc. 39) granting Am Purpose’s motion to
vacate the warrant of arrest. 3  The reasons set out in that opinion support granting summary
judgment now to dismiss the in rem claims and Rule C arrest of the vessel.
Accordingly,
IT IS ORDERED that Am Purpose’s motion for partial summary judgment (R. Doc. 37) is
GRANTED for the reasons stated in the Court’s Order &  Reasons vacating the warrant for arrest
(R. Doc. 39).
New Orleans, Louisiana, this 18th day of September, 2026.
