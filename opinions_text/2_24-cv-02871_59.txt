sufficient, even in the absence of new evidence or an intervening change in or
clarification of the substantive law.”19  This Court has held that, “the correct starting

Porter has failed to show that reconsideration of the Court’s September 29,

“for any reason it deems sufficient, even in the absence of new evidence or an
intervening change in or clarification of the substantive law,” 28 the Court finds no
sufficient reason exists to do so.  The Court therefore finds that Porter’s Motion does
not meet the standards set forth by Fed. R. Civ. P. 59(e), and must be denied.
