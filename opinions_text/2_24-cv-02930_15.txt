ORDER
 The Court, after considering the complaint, the record, the applicable
law, the Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation, and finding that as
of this date Plaintiff has filed no objections to the Magistrate Judge’s Report
and Recommendation, hereby approves the Magistrate Judge’s Report and
Recommendation and adopts it as its opinion.  Accordingly,
IT IS ORDERED that Plaintiff’s civil action under 42 U.S.C. § 1983 be
TRANSFERRED to the United States District Court for the Central District
of California Western Division for further proceedings.
