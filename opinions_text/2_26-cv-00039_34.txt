FED. R. CIV. P. 54(B) JUDGMENT
The Court having approved the Report and Recommendation the United States Magistrate
Judge and having adopted it as its opinion herein, and finding no just cause for delay, accordingly,
 IT IS ORDERED, ADJUDGED, AND DECREED that Bobby Rand Golden’s § 1983
claims against Sergeant Ford, “Jefferson Parish Correctional Center Medical Personnel,” and
“Jefferson Parish Correctional Center Security Administration, as well as his § 1983 claims of
unlawful arrest, excessive force during search/arrest, and unlawful seizure of vehicle against
Detective Charles Carollo, Detective Carl Marshall, Detective Patrick Evans, Sergeant Josh
Bermudez, Derek Green, Keith Branchcomb, Blake Holliefield, Sergeant Julio Alvarado, are
DISMISSED WITH PREJUDICE.
IT IS FURTHER ORDERED, ADJUDGED, AND DECREED that Golden’s § 1983
claims of food tampering, foul language, and deliberate indifference to serious medical needs, and
his request that defendants be criminally prosecuted are DISMISSED WITH PREJUDICE.
IT IS FURTHER ORDERED, ADJUDGED, AND DECREED that Golden’s request
for habeas relief is DISMISSED WITHOUT PREJUDICE.
