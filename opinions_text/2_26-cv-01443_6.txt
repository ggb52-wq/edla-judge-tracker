 Before the Court is plaintiff Colby J.  Kinsey’s petition for issuance of a
writ of habeas corpus. 1  On July 29, 2026, Magistrate Judge North issued a
Report & Recommendation (R&R), recommending that the captioned matter
be transferred to the United States District Court for the Western District of
Louisiana.2  No party objected to the R&R.  Therefore, the Court reviews the
R&R for clear error.  See Douglass v. United Servs. Auto. Ass’n, 79 F.3d 1415,
1430 (5th Cir. 1996) (en banc), superseded by statute on other grounds ,
28 U.S.C. § 636(b)(1); see also Fed. R. Civ. P. 72(b ) Advisory Committee’s
note (1983) (“When no timely objectio n is filed, the court need only satisfy
itself that there is no clear error on th e face of the record in order to accept
the recommendation.”).
