As other orders in this case have noted, 11 and as plaintiff himself concedes, 12
defendant has not yet been served in this matter  and it has not yet entered an

appear on the  docket once service is effectuated by the U.S. Marshal s Service. 13
Plaintiff may refile appropriate motions at that time.
With respect to his motions requesting that all prior medical filings be sealed,
plaintiff has not identified which filings or portions thereof he wishes to be filed under
seal.14 The Court cannot meaningfully “review each document to decide which contain
sufficient personal information to outweigh any public interest in sealing what is
