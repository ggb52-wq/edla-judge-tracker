Plaintiff Telly Savlaes Ambrose brought claims pursuant to 42 U.S.C.
§ 1983 against the Jefferson Parish Detention Center, Jefferson Parish
Medical Direct Health, Sheriff Joseph P. Lopinto, III, and Chief Sue Ellen
Monfra.1  On March 2, 2023, Magistrate Judge Michael B. North issued a
Report and Recommendation (the “R&R”) in which he recommends
that this Court dismiss plaintiff’s claims against the Jefferson Parish
Detention Center with prejudice on the grounds that it “is not a person or
suable entity to be held liable under Section 1983.” 2
Plaintiff did not object to the R&R.  Therefore, this Court reviews the
R&R for clear error.  See Douglass v. United Servs. Auto. Ass’n, 79 F.3d 1415,
1430 (5th Cir. 1996) (en banc), superseded by statute on other grounds, 28
U.S.C. § 636(b)(1); see also Fed. R. Civ. P. 72(b) advisory committee’s note

Accordingly, the Court orders plaintiff’s claims against the Jefferson
