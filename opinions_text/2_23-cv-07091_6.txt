The Court, having considered the record, the applicable law, the Report and
Recommendation1 of the Magistrate Judge, and the failure2 of the Plaintiff Jessica L.
Seamon to file an objection to the Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation,

it as its opinion in this matter.
Accordingly,
IT IS ORDERED  that Plaintiff’s complaint be DISMISSED WITHOUT
PREJUDICE pursuant to Rule 4(m) of the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure.
New Orleans, Louisiana, this 17th day of June, 2025.
