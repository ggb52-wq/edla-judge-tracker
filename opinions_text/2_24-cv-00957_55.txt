North’s well-reasoned R&R.  Porter simply rehashes his complaint, and cites
to an RCC Directive and a Rule for the Corrections Employee Manual that he
believes provide support for his complaint.  Nothing in it addresses
Magistrate Judge North’s correct determinations that plaint iff is unable to
meet the standard for a successful exce ssive use of force claim, that any
attempt by plaintiff to amend the complaint would be futile, and that plaintiff
is not entitled to a default judgment.
