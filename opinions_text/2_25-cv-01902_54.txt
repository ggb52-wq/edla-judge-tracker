UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT
EASTERN DISTRICT OF LOUISIANA

CALVIONA LYNISE BROWN CIVIL ACTION

VERSUS No. 25-1902

EPIC FEDERAL CREDIT UNION, ET AL. SECTION I

ORDER
The Court, having considered the complaint, the record, the applicable law,
and the Report and Recommendation of the United States Magistrate Judge,1 hereby
approves the Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation and adopts it as its
opinion in this matter.  Therefore,
IT IS ORDERED  that plaintiff Calviona Lynise Brown’s claims against
defendants Jade Ealy, High Level Hearing Technology, LLC, and Lana Joseph are
DISMISSED WITHOUT PREJUDICE pursuant to Fed. R. Civ. P. 21.
New Orleans, Louisiana, May 22, 2026.
