O R D E R
The Court, having considered the complaint, the record, the applicable law, the Report and
Recommendation of the United States Magistrate Judge, and the failure of any party to file an
objection to the Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation, hereby approves the Magistrate
Judge’s Report and Recommendation and adopts it as its opinion in this matter.  Therefore,
IT IS ORDERED that Herman L. Scott’s 42 U.S.C. § 1983 claims against defendant Lt.
LeBlanc be DISMISSED WITH PREJUDICE  pursuant to 28 U.S.C. § 1915, § 1915A and as
applicable, 42 U.S.C. § 1997e, as frivolous and otherwise for failure  to state a claim for which
relief can be granted until the conditions under Heck v. Humphrey, 512 U.S. 477 (1994), are met.
New Orleans, Louisiana, this 22nd day of February, 2023.
