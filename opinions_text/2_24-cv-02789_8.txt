O R D E R
The Court, having considered the complaint, the record, the applicable law, the Report and
Recommendation of the United States Magistrate Judge  (R. Doc. 7), and the failure of any party
to file an objection to the Magistrate Judge ’s Report and Recommendation, hereby approves the
Report and Recommendation of the United States Magistrate Judge and adopts it as its opinion in
this matter.  Therefore,
IT IS ORDERED that plaintiff Cody Dantin’s  civil action under 42 U.S.C. § 1983 is
TRANSFERRED to the United States District Court for the Middle  District of Louisiana  for
further proceedings.
New Orleans, Louisiana, this 21st day of February 2025.
