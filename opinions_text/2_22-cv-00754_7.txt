ORDER
The Court, having considered the Complaint, the record, the applicable law,
the Report and Recommendation of the United States Magistrate Judge ,1 and the
Plaintiff’s failure to file an objection to the Magistrate Judge’s Report and
Recommendation,2 hereby approves the Report and Recommendation of the United
States Magistrate Judge and adopts it as its opinion in this matter.
Accordingly,
IT IS HEREBY ORDERED that Plaintiff’s Complaint seeking relief
pursuant to 42 U.S.C. § 19833 is DISMISSED WITH PREJUDICE as malicious.
