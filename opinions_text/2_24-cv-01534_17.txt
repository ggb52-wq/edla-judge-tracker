ORDER

 The Court, having considered the complaint, the record, the applicable law, the
Report and Recommendation of the United States Magistrate Judge (Rec. Doc. 1 6),
and the failure of any party to file an objection to the Magistrate Judge’s Report and
Recommendation, hereby approves the Report and Recommendation of the United
States Magistrate Judge and adopts it as its opinion in this matter.  Therefore,
  IT IS ORDERED that Defendant’s unopposed Motion to Reverse and Remand
Complaint (Rec. Doc. 14) is GRANTED, and judgment is entered REVERSING the
Administrative Law Judge’s decision and REMANDING the case for further
administrative proceedings.
