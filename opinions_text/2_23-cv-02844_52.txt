ORDER
 Plaintiff Steve Ricks filed this lawsuit against defendant Imperial Fire
and Casualty Insurance Company in July 2023 .1  Chief Magistrate Judge
Michael North issued a Report and Recommendation (“R&R”) in which he
recommends that the Court issue a 60 -day order of dismissal  because the
case has been settled between the parties.2
 No party objected to the R&R.  The  Court thus reviews the R&R for
clear error.  See Douglass v. United Servs. Auto. Ass ’n, 79 F.3d 1415, 1430
(5th Cir. 1996) (en banc), superseded by statute on other grounds, 28 U.S.C.
§ 636(b)(1); see also Fed. R. Civ. P. 72(b) advisory committee’s note ( 1983)
(“When no timely objection is filed, the court need only satisfy itself that
there is no clear error on the face of the record in order to accept the
recommendation.”).  The Court finds no clear error.  Therefore , the Court
adopts the R&R as its opinion.
