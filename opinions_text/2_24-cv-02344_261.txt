summary judgment with conclusory allegations, unsubstantiated assertions, or “only
a scintilla of evidence.”88  Instead, summary judgment is appropriate if a reasonable
jury could not return a verdict for the nonmoving party.89

of proof at trial, the moving party “must come forward with evidence which would
entitle it to a directed verdict if the evidence went uncontroverted at trial.”90  The
