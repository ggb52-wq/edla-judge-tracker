ORDER
The Court, after considering the complaint, the record, the applicable law, the Magistrate
Judge’s Report and Recommendation (R. Doc. 5), and finding that as of this date the parties have
filed no objections to the Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation, hereby approves the
Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation and adopts it as its opinion.  Accordingly,
 IT IS ORDERED that Plaintiff’s Complaint is DISMISSED  for failure to state a claim
on which relief may be granted pursuant to 28 U.S.C. § 1915(e)(2)(B).
