Before the Court is Defendants Talos Production, Inc., improperly named as Talos Energy,
Inc. (“Talos Energy”) in the Petition, and Talos Energy’s  (collectively, “Talos”) Motion for
Judgment on the Pleadings. 1 Talos asserts Plaintiff’s open account claim should be dismissed

in opposition, the record, and the applicable law, the Court grant s the motion to the extent it
requests the Court find that maritime law governs this dispute. The Court also grants Plaintiff leave
to file an amended complaint.
