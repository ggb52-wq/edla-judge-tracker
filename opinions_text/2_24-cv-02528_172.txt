ORDER & REASONS
Before the Court is the motion of plaintiff Charles George for reconsideration of this
Court’s July 29, 2026 Order & Reasons awarding him $128,375 in attorney’s fees.1  George argues
that this Court erred in its determination of the lodestar and its application of the factors set out in
Johnson v. Georgia Highway Express, Inc., 488 F.2d 714, 717-19 (5th Cir. 1974), for awarding
attorney’s fees when it reduced his requested attorn ey’s fees fro m $371,600 to $128,375. 2
Defendant Hobby Lobby Stores, Inc. (“Hobby Lobby”) responds in opposition, explaining in a
clear and cogent fashion that George’s motion for reconsideration merely rehashes the arguments
this Court previously considered and rejected in its 14-page Order & Reasons fixing the attorney’s
fees award.3  In reply, George reiterates how he th inks the Court erred and where he believes
Hobby Lobby failed to respond to his arguments.4
Largely for the reasons fully articulated by Hobby Lobby in its opposition, the Court finds
no reason to alter its prior order fixing George’s attorney’s fees at $128,375.  George’s arguments
were previously considered in full and mostly ( but not entirely) rejected by the Court.  George’s
counsel is simply dissatisfied w ith the Court’s ruling and fails to  recognize that the relief he
