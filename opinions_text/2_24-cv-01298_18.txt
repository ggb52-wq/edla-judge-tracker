ORDER AND REASONS
 Before the Court is Plaintiff’s action for review of a final decision of the
Acting Commissioner of Social Security denying Plaintiff’s claim for
disability insurance benefits.  On July 14, 2025, Chief Magistrate Judge
North issued an R&R, recommending that the Court dismiss Plaintiff’s case
with prejudice.1  Plaintiff did not object to the R&R.  Therefore, the Court
reviews the R&R for clear error.  See Douglass v. United Servs. Auto. Ass’n,
79 F.3d 1415, 1430 (5th Cir. 1996) (en banc), superseded by statute on other
grounds, 28 U.S.C. § 636(b)(1); see also  Fed. R. Civ. P. 72(b) advisory
committee’s note (1983) (“When no timely objection is filed, the court need
only satisfy itself that there is no clear error on the face of the record in order
to accept the recommendation.”).
 The Court finds no clear error.  Accordingly, the Court adopts the
Magistrate Judge’s R&R as its opinion.  The Court REJECTS Plaintiff’s brief,
