ORDER
The Court, after considering the complaint, the record, the applicable law, the
Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation (Rec. Doc. 5) and finding that as of
this date the parties have filed no objections to the Magistrate Judge’s Report and
Recommendation, hereby approves the Magistrate Judge’s Report and
Recommendation and adopts it as its opinion.  Accordingly,
 IT IS HEREBY ORDERED that Plaintiff’s Complaint is DISMISSED for
lack of subject-matter jurisdiction under 28 U.S.C. § 1915(e)(2)(B).
New Orleans, Louisiana, this 8th day of January, 2025.
