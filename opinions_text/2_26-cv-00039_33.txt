ORDER

The Court, having considered the complaint, the record, the applicable law, the Report and
Recommendation of the United States Magistrate Judge, R. Doc. 31, and the failure of Plaintiff to
file an objection to the Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation, hereby approves the
Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation and adopts it as its opinion herein. Therefore,
The Court having approved the Report and Recommendation of the United States
Magistrate Judge and having adopted it as its opinion herein; accordingly,
 IT IS ORDERED that  Bobby Rand Golden’s § 1983 claims against Sergeant Ford,
“Jefferson Parish Correctional Center Medical Personnel,” and “Jefferson Parish Correctional
Center Security Administration, as well as his § 1983 claims of unlawful arrest, excessive force
during search/arrest, and unlawful seizure of vehicle against Detective Charles Carollo, Detective
Carl Marshall, Detective Patrick Evans, Sergeant Josh Bermudez, Derek Green, Keith
Branchcomb, Blake Holliefield, Sergeant Julio Alvarado, are DISMISSED WITH PREJUDICE
as they are frivolous and/or fail to state a claim on which relief may be granted.
IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that Golden’s § 1983 claims of food tampering, foul
language, and deliberate indifference to serious medical needs, and his request that defendants be
criminally prosecuted are DISMISSED WITH PREJUDICE as they are frivolous and/or fail to
state a claim on which relief may be granted.

IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that Golden’s request for habeas relief is  DISMISSED
WITHOUT PREJUDICE to as legally frivolous and for seeking relief that cannot be granted in
a § 1983 civil rights proceeding.
IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that Golden’s claim for false imprisonment against
Detective Charles Carollo, Detective Carl Marshall, Detective Patrick Evans, Sergeant Josh
Bermudez, Derek Green, Keith Branchcomb, Blake Holliefield, Sergeant Julio Alvarado are
STAYED.
   IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that the Clerk of Court mark this action CLOSED for
statistical purposes.
IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that within thirty (30) days after the finality of Golden’s
conviction or upon disposition of the charges in his favor, Golden may move to reopen the matter
for further proceedings consistent with Heck. The failure timely to file the motion to reopen could
waive Golden’s opportunity to proceed with this civil action.
