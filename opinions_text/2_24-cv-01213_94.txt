On May 5, 2025, Magistrate Judge Eva J. Dossier issued a Report and
Recommendation (“R&R”), recommendi ng that plaintiff’s complaint be
dismissed without prejudice for failure to prosecute.  Plaintiff did not object
to the R&R.  Therefore, the Court reviews the R&R for clear error.  See
Douglass v. United Servs. Auto. Ass’n, 79 F.3d 1415, 1430 (5th Cir. 1996) (en
banc), superseded by statut e on other grounds , 28 U.S.C. § 636(b)(1); see
also Fed. R. Civ. P. 72(b) advisory committee’s note (1983) (“When no timely
objection is filed, the court need only satisfy itself that there is no clear error
on the face of the record in order to  accept the recommendation.”).  The
Court finds no clear error.
Accordingly, the Court adopts Magi strate Judge Dossier’s R&R as its
opinion.  Strausbaugh’s petition is DISMISSED WITHOUT PREJUDICE.
