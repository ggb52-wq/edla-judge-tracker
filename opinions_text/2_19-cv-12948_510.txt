review de novo those portions of the report and recommendations to which a specific
objection is made. 60   The Court reviews all other portions of the report and
recommendations for plain error.61

III. ANALYSIS
A. Attorney’s Fees

1. The Williamson Factors

Louisiana’s Open Account Statute, La. R.S. 9:2781 , allows for the recovery of
“reasonable attorney fees.”  The Fifth Circuit has held that, “State law controls both
the award of and the reasonableness of fees awarded where state law supplies the
rule of decision.” 62  In determining a reasonable award of attorney’s fees under La.
R.S. 9:2781 , Louisiana courts generally consider the following ten factors: (1) the
ultimate result obtained; (2) the responsibility incurred; (3) the importance of the
litigation; (4) the amount of money involved; (5) the extent and character of the work
performed; (6) the legal knowledge , attainment, and skill of the attorney; (7) the
number of appearances made;  (8) the intricacies of facts involved; (9) the diligence
