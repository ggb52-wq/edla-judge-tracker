ORDER
The Court, having considered the reco rd, the applicable law, the Report and
Recommendation1 of the United States Magistrate Judge, and the failure 2 of the
Defendant Vincent Piazza, Jr. and Sons Seafood, Inc. to file an objection to the
Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendation, hereby approves the Report and
Recommendation of the Magistrate Judge and adopts it as its opinion in this matter.
Accordingly,
IT IS ORDERED that motion3 for reasonable attorneys’ fees be GRANTED.
Reasonable attorneys’ fees in the amount of $105,100.88 are awarded to Plaintiffs
BlueTeam Roofing, LLC successor-by-assign ment to Sendero Restoration Services,
LLC and Sendero Restoration Services, LLC (“Sendero”) and against Defendant
Vincent Piazza, Jr. & Sons Seafood, Inc.
IT IS FURTHER ORDERED  that Sendero be awarded nontaxable costs in
the amount of $1,324.68.
