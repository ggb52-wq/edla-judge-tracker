judgment.”  Harcon Barge Co. v. D & G Boat Rentals, Inc. , 784 F.2d 665,
669 (5th Cir. 1986).  Despite the si milarities, one difference between the
two is Rule 59(e) has a prescribed time limit: “A motion to alter or amend a
judgment must be filed no later th an 28 days after the entry of the
judgment.”  Fed. R. Civ. P. 59(e).  “Under which Rule the motion falls turns

defendant USAA may have cured the juris dictional defect before the Court
dismissed the case, the motion is m oot once the case was dismissed.  See
