The Court, after  considering  the  complaint,  the  record,  the  applicable  law,  the
Magistrate Judge’s Findings and  Recommendation, 1 and  finding  that  as  of  this  date
Plaintiff has filed no objections to the Magistrate Judge’s Findings and Recommendation,
approves the  Ma gistrate Judge ’s Findings  and  Recommendation  and  adopts  it  as  its
opinion. Accordingly,
IT IS ORDERED  that Robertson’s Motion to Lift Stay 2 is GRANTED, and the
previously imposed stay LIFTED.
IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that  Robertson’s complaint asserting individual
claims against Candies and Caruso pursuant to 42 U.S.C. § 1983 is DISMISSED WITH
PREJUDICE until such time as the conditions of Heck are met.
