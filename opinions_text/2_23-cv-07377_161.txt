Plaintiff Godfrey’s Motion to Break Statute of Limitations (ECF N o. 160) is DENIED.
While the court is sympathetic to Mr. Godfrey’s personal hardships, such hardships do not provide
a basis for tolling an otherwise applicable statute of limitations.  See Adhikari v. Kellogg Brown &
Root, Inc., 845 F.3d 184, 207 (5th Cir. 2017).  Nor has Plaintiff alleged facts supporting a finding
that extraordinary circumstances prevented him from timely filing and he pursued his rights
diligently, that he was misled or tricked by his adversary into allowing the deadline to pass, or that
the EEOC misled him about the nature of his rights to file suit.  See In re Deepwater Horizon, No.
20-30673, 2021 WL 4888395, at* 2 (5 th Cir. Oct. 19, 2021); Heimeshoff v. Hartford Life &
Accident Insurance Co ., 571 U.S. 99, 114 (2013); Irwin v. Department of Veterans Affairs , 498
U.S. 89, 96 & nn. 3-4 (1990); Bernstein v. Maximus Federal Services, Inc., 63 F.4th 967, 970 (5th
Cir. 2023).
