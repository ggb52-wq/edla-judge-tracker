ORDER AND REASONS

Considering the Court’s previous order (Rec. Doc. 20),
On May 17, 2023, counsel for plaintiff Stacy L. Boudreaux
filed a motion to withdraw from the above captioned matter. Rec.
Doc. 19. The Court granted the motion and withdrew L. Eric
Williams, Jr. (La. Bar No. 26773) and Keith E. Patton from the

counsel in this action; notice should be sent to the Clerk of
Court, 500 Poydras St., New Orleans, LA 70130.” Id. at 1-2.
Finally, the Court cautioned “Failure to timely comply with this
order, other court ordered deadlines, and failure to timely review
the Clerk of Court record in this action might lead to dismissal
of complaint.” Id. To date, plaintiff has neither enrolled new
