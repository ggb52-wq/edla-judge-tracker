ORDER
  The Court, having considered the record, applicable law, the Report and Recommendation
of the United States Magistrate Judge, and the failure of the plaintiff to file an objection to the
Magistrate Judge’s Report and Recommendatio n, hereby approves the Report and
Recommendation of the United States Magistrate Judge and adopts it as its opinion in this matter.
Therefore,
IT IS ORDERED that Marlowe E. Parker’s § 1983 claims against the State of Louisiana,
St. Tammany Parish Sheriffs Office, and St. Tammany Parish Sheriff Randy Smith are
DISMISSED WITH PREJUDICE as frivolous and for failure to state a claim upon which relief
can be granted pursuant to 28 U.S.C. § 1915(e), § 1915A, and 42 U.S.C. § 1997e.
New Orleans, Louisiana, this 23rd day of February, 2024.
