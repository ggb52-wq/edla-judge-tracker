ORDER
 Plaintiff Percell James Matthews brings this action pro se under 42
U.S.C. § 1983.1  On January 4, 2022, Magistrate Judge Dana M. Douglas
issued a Report & Recommendation (“R&R”), recommending that plaintiff’s
complaint be dismissed without prejudice for failure to prosecute.2
 No party objected to the R&R.  Therefore, th e Court reviews the R&R
for clear error.  Douglass v. United Servs. Auto. Ass’n , 79 F.3d 1415, 1430
(5th Cir. 1996); see also  Fed. R. Civ. P. 72(b) advisory committee’s note
(1983) (“When no timely objection is filed, the court need only satisfy itself
that there is no clear error on the face of the record in order to accept the
recommendation.”).  The Court finds no clear error.
