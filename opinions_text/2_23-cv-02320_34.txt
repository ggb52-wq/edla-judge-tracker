REPORT AND RECOMMENDATION

Given this Court’s Order and Recommendation in Ricks v. Imperial Fire & Casualty
Co., Civ. A. No. 23 -2844 (E.D. La., Dec. 12, 2024) and its adoption by the en banc District
Court, In re: Hurricane Ida Cases, Order Regarding Settlement of Former MMA Cases  (E.D. La.,
Dec. 19, 2024),1 the Court re-issues the following:
Before the Court is Kin Interinsurance Network’s Motion for Judgment of Default
Against McClenny, Moseley & Associates, PLLC (“MMA”).  (R ec. doc. 30) .  There is no
opposition to the motion.
The Court notes that the Clerk of Court has entered default against MMA in this
lawsuit.  Moreover, the Court has previously found (in an un- appealed Order in a separate
case) that MMA has knowingly and completely waived its rights to intervene or otherwise
seek to recover fees and/or costs in any lawsuit pending in this District, based upon its
affirmative representations to the Court that it “does not intend to intervene in any cases
pending in this Court to seek attorneys’ fees or costs.”  See Ricks v. Imperial Fire & Cas. Co.,
Civ. A. No. 23 -2844, 2024 WL 1750738 (E.D. La. Apr. 5, 2024) , report and recommendation
adopted, No. CV 23-2844, 2024 WL 1740172 (E.D. La. Apr. 23, 2024).

 A party’s failure to file written objections to the proposed findings, conclusions, and

from attacking on appeal the unobjected-to proposed factual findings and legal conclusions

such consequences will result from a failure to object.  Douglass v. United States Auto. Assoc.,
