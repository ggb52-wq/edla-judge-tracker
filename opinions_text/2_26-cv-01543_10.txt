ORDER AND REASONS
 Before the Court is  Plaintiffs’ Unopposed Motion and Incorporated
Memorandum in Support of Motion for Leave to File Supplemental and Amended
Complaint filed by Plaintiff Tony Washington.1
While Plaintiff has properly alleged his  own citizenship,2 and the citizenship
of defendant Caesars Entertainment, Inc., 3 he has failed to properly allege the
citizenship of defendant Jazz Casino Company, L.L.C.4  With respect to Jazz Casino
Company, L.L.C., Plaintiff must  identify each of the members of the limited liability
company and then must properly allege the citizenship of each member in accordance
with the requirements of 28 U.S.C. § 1332(a) and (c).5 The same requirement applies
to any member of a limited liability company which is also a limited liability
company.6 Proper information regarding the citizenship of all parties and the amount
in controversy is necessary to establish the Court’s diversity jurisdiction.

Accordingly,
IT IS HE REBY ORDERED that Plaintiff’s Motion for Leave to File

IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that plaintiff, Tony Washington, shall have ten

1332(a). The comprehensive amended pleading shall include all of P laintiff’s
