ORDER & REASONS
Before the Court is a  Motion for Partial Dismissal pursuant to Federal  Rule of Civil
Procedure 12(b)(6) filed by Defendant AmeriGas Propane, LP (“AmeriGas”). R. Doc. 18. Therein,
AmeriGas asks the Court  to dismiss  Plaintiff Charles Walker’s (“Walker”) reasonable
accommodation and disparate treatment claims under the Americans with Disabilities  Act
(“ADA”) for failure to state a claim pursuant to Rule 12(b)(6).
Walker’s opposition memorandum was originally due on October 6, 2026. Walker did not
file an opposition by that date, and instead, asked for an extension of time to respond. R. Doc. 19.
The Court granted Walker’s extension and gave him until the end of the day on October 20,
2026, to file an opposition. R. Doc. 22. Walker missed that deadline and, as of the date of this
Order, has not contacte d the Court, explaine d his absence, or asked for leave to file an untimely
opposition.1
The Court has freely granted Walker the opportunity to submit an opposition to
AmeriGas’s pending motion late, and Walker has failed to take that opportunity. Accordingly,
the Court construes AmeriGas’s Motion for Partial Dismissal as unopposed.
1 This is not the first time Walker has missed a filing deadline. The Court previously granted Walker leave to
file an opposition memorandum to an earlier motion to dismiss, R. Doc. 7, six days late.
