Civil Procedure filed by defendant Deborah Weisler Fallis. 1  The motion was set for submission
on January 9, 2025.2  Local Rule 7.5 of the United States District Court for the Eastern District of
Louisiana requires that a memorandum in opposition to a motion be filed no later than eight days
before the noticed submission date, making the deadline in this instance January 2, 2025.3  Plaintiff
Tyrone Clofer, who is not represented by counsel, 4 did not file an opposition to the motion.
