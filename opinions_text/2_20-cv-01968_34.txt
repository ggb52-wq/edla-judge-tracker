RONALD NICHOLAS, JR.      CIVIL ACTION

VERSUS         NO.  20-1968

SOCIAL SECURITY ADMINISTRATION    SECTION M (5)

ORDER

 Having considered the parties’ motions for summary judgment, 1 the record, the applicable
law, the magistrate judge’s Report and Recommendation (“R&R”), 2 and the failure of plaintiff
Ronald Nicholas, Jr., to file a ny objections to the R&R, the Court hereby approves the R&R and
adopts it as its opinion herein.  Accordingly,
 IT IS ORDERED that Nicholas’s motion for summary judgment is denied, that defendant
Social Security Administration’s motion for summary judgment is granted, and that Nicholas’s
suit is dismissed with prejudice.
 New Orleans, Louisiana, this 17 th day of March, 2023.
