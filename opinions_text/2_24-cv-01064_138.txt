REPORT AND RECOMMENDATION

Before the Court is Defendants’ Joint Motion for Attorney Fees and Costs
Pursuant to Fed. R. Civ. P. 54(d) and La. R.S. 51:1409(A) (R. Doc. 134). Plaintiff
opposes the motion. For the following reasons, it is recommended that the motion be
denied.
“Upon a finding by the court that an action under [the Louisiana Unfair Trade
Practices Act (“LUTPA”)] was groundless and brought in bad faith or for purposes of
harassment, the court may award to the defendant reasonable attorney fees and
costs.” La. R.S. 51:1409(a). “ The court notes that this award is discretionary. ”
Spillway Invs., L.L.C. v. Pilot Travel Centers, LLC , No. CIV.A. 04 -2451, 2006 WL
2524163, at *1 (E.D. La. Aug. 28, 2006)  (citing Hill Wholesale Distributing Co., Inc.
v. Louis W. Howat & Son, 666 So.2d 1252 (La. App. 2d Cir.1996)). “Moreover, simply
because a plaintiff’s claim is not successful [] does not necessarily warrant an award
of attorneys’ fees and costs herein.” Id.
“LUTPA is, on its face, somewhat vague and quite broad in the sense that it
prohibits generally ‘ unfair methods of competition and unfair or deceptive acts or
practices in the conduct of any trade or commerce .’” Id. Here, Plaintiff alleged that

The motion also fails because Defendants have not established that Plaintiff’s
purpose was to harass them or that he acted in bad faith. Defendants rely primarily

with the claims for breach of contract and quantum meruit, the instant case and the
SDTX litigation are not based on the same nucleus of operative facts.”3 Further, the
