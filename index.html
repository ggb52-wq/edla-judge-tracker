    How a motion is matched to a ruling

    In this court a motion is set for submission on a date, and after that date it is in the judge's hands. The site records every civil motion from the court's public feed and reads each motion's submission date from the docket text. "No ruling found" means no order in the same case naming that type of motion has appeared since the motion was filed. That is a docket match, not a certainty: a motion withdrawn, decided under a different docket event, or ruled on by minute entry can still show here. How we count
