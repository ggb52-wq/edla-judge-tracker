    Which motions are past their submission date with no ruling on the docket yet?

    Submitted motions

    In this court a motion is set for submission on a date, and after that date it is in the judge's hands. The site records every civil motion from the court's public feed since — and reads each motion's submission date from the docket text; motions filed before that date are not here. "No ruling found" means no order in the same case naming that type of motion has appeared since the motion was filed. That is a docket match, not a certainty: a motion withdrawn, decided under a different docket event, or ruled on by a minute entry can still show here. Motions whose submission date has not been read yet are listed without one.
