                                        Cases

                                        Likely and possible

                                        Likely: the nature of suit or cause is the Defend Trade Secrets Act, or docket text or a written opinion names a trade-secret statute, La. R.S. 23:921, or a noncompete or non-solicitation agreement. Possible: a motion for a TRO, preliminary injunction, or expedited discovery in a contract case, or in a case with no nature of suit on file that names no government party. The court's feed shows these motions within minutes of filing; the complaint's claims are not in any free source. How we find them
